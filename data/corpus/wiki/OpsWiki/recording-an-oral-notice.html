WI-005 — Recording an oral notice

Type: Work instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Status at issue: Active
Effective/issued date: 2017-04-01
Version: 1

Purpose

Use this page to record an oral notice in the dispute case file when a customer contacts Harborline by phone and provides notice of an error, unauthorized transfer, or card dispute before written confirmation is received. Record the notice promptly and consistently so downstream investigators have a complete intake record. This procedure supports DOP-202 and the intake requirements in DOP-201.

Before you start

  • Confirm you have the customer’s dispute claim number, if one already exists.
  • Verify the caller identity using the standard authentication steps in the contact center or operations script.
  • Keep the relevant account open in CardView and the case record open in ClaimTrack.
  • If the claim involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card transaction, be prepared to use Corvane portal to review processor details.
  • Do not promise a final outcome or timeline while recording the notice.

Procedure

  1. Open the case in ClaimTrack.
    Search by claim number, customer name, or account number. If the claim does not yet exist, create the intake record using the customer’s oral report and the available account details.

  2. Review the account in CardView.
    Confirm the card product, last four digits, and current status. Use CardView to verify whether the item concerns debit, credit, or prepaid activity and to capture any visible transaction reference points.

  3. Check processor details in Corvane portal, if applicable.
    For card transactions, open the matching card record and review the transaction list, authorization data, or chargeback-related indicators available in Corvane portal. Note any merchant name variation, terminal information, or reversal marker that may help classify the claim.

  4. Select the oral notice entry type in ClaimTrack.
    In the case activity panel, choose the oral notice option. Enter the contact date, contact time, and the channel as phone. Use the exact date and time of the call received.

  5. Document the customer statement.
    Summarize the notice in neutral language. Capture:

  6. the transaction date and amount, if known
  7. the merchant, ATM, transfer, or account reference
  8. whether the customer alleges an error, unauthorized transfer, or card dispute

  9. any immediate facts provided by the customer, including duplicate transaction, no receipt, cash not received, card not present, or account compromise

  10. Record affected account and product information.
    Include the deposit account, debit card, credit card, or prepaid card involved. If the customer mentions more than one item, enter each item separately in the same case.

  11. Add supporting notes from CardView and Corvane portal.
    Enter only facts confirmed from the systems. Do not interpret liability, merchant responsibility, or coverage at this stage.

  12. Save the case and confirm the audit trail.
    Save all changes in ClaimTrack. Reopen the activity history to confirm the oral notice entry displays correctly and the case shows the contact as recorded.

  13. Route the claim if additional handling is required.
    If the notice indicates a disputed debit card point-of-sale item, ATM transaction, ACH debit, credit card billing error, or prepaid transaction, route the case to the appropriate investigation queue under the related DOP procedure.

Tip

Use plain, factual wording. A good entry states what the customer said and what systems show, without adding judgment or assumptions.

Warning

If the customer reports urgent fraud indicators, account takeover, or repeated unauthorized activity, follow the applicable fraud referral procedure in addition to recording the oral notice.

Note

Oral notice entries are part of the official case record and may be reviewed later for quality assurance, complaint handling, or customer correspondence.
