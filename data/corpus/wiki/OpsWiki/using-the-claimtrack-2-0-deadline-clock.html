Using the ClaimTrack 2.0 deadline clock

WI-012 — Using the ClaimTrack 2.0 deadline clock

Type: Work Instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective / issued date: 2023-06-05
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this work instruction to check, interpret, and update the automated deadline clock in ClaimTrack 2.0 for consumer disputes and claims. ClaimTrack 2.0 became the production case-management system for Disputes Operations in June 2023 and replaces the prior manual deadline tracking method used in the legacy ClaimTrack environment.

This page supports the procedures in CPS-SOP-210 — Investigation Timelines and Extensions, CPS-SOP-211 — Provisional Credit, CPS-SOP-212 — Final Determination and Customer Notices, and CPS-SOP-231 — Business Day Calculation and Holiday Calendar.

[!NOTE]

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim is open in ClaimTrack 2.0 and not in a closed, voided, or duplicate status.
  • Have the customer’s card number, claim number, and processing channel available.
  • Use CardView for account verification before making any deadline edits.
  • Use the Corvane Card Services portal only when the claim involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card transaction routed through Corvane.
  • If the clock does not display or appears frozen, stop and follow the escalation path in JA-130 — Escalation Contacts.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  2. Sign in to ClaimTrack using your standard network credentials.
  3. From the home queue, search by claim number, customer name, or last four digits of the card.

  4. Open the case from the results list.

  5. Locate the deadline clock panel.

  6. On the claim header, select Timeline.
  7. Review the Deadline Clock field.

  8. Confirm the clock is showing the active stage, such as intake, provisional credit, investigation, final determination, or extension review.

  9. Verify the source data before relying on the clock.

  10. Select Case Details.
  11. Confirm the claim type, received date, and channel match the intake record.

  12. If the claim is tied to a card transaction, open CardView in a second window and verify the card status, account open date, and any recent maintenance activity.

  13. Check for external routing impacts.

  14. If the case involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card transaction, open the Corvane Card Services portal.
  15. Review the transaction response history, chargeback status, and any pending network events.

  16. Return to ClaimTrack and confirm the deadline clock reflects the current status shown in Corvane.

  17. Advance the claim stage if the next action is complete.

  18. In ClaimTrack, select Update Status.
  19. Choose the next processing step from the dropdown list.
  20. Save the case.

  21. The automated clock should refresh after save and display the updated deadline.

  22. Add a case note when the clock changes.

  23. Open Notes.
  24. Enter the reason for the status change, the screen used to verify it, and the employee ID or team name if a handoff occurred.

  25. Keep the note factual and concise.

  26. Escalate exceptions immediately.

  27. If the clock shows an unexpected extension, missing stage, or conflicting due date, do not overwrite the field manually.
  28. Capture the screen details, notify your lead, and follow the escalation route in JA-130.
  29. For policy-related exceptions, route the matter through the applicable SOP owner.

[!TIP] Use the deadline clock as the primary visual indicator, but always validate it against the case history. A correct clock with incorrect source data can still produce the wrong processing outcome.

[!WARNING] Do not use the old manual deadline worksheet from the shared drive for active ClaimTrack 2.0 cases. The worksheet may be retained for reference only and does not control case timing.

Common checks

Situation What to confirm
Clock does not display Case is open, user has correct role, page refreshed
Clock shows the wrong stage Case history, status transition, last saved action
Clock changed after card research Corvane response posted and case saved
Clock appears overdue Received date, business day calculation, holiday calendar
