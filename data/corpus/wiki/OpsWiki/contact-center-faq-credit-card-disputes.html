Purpose

Use this FAQ when a customer contacts the Contact Center about a credit card billing error or unauthorized credit card charge. This job aid supports DOP-209 — Credit Card Billing Error Disputes, DOP-225 — Claims Received by the Contact Center, DOP-210 — Investigation Timelines and Extensions, and DOP-230 — Customer Letters and Notices.

Quick answers

Customer question Approved response / action
“Can I dispute this credit card charge?” Customers have 90 days from the charge date to dispute a credit card charge.
“Do I need to call or can I submit it another way?” Take the claim in Genesys, confirm the key facts, and route it to Claims Intake for case setup in ClaimTrack.
“What information do you need?” Cardholder name, last 4 digits of the card, charge date, merchant name, amount, and the reason for the dispute.
“Will my card be closed?” Do not promise account changes. If fraud indicators are present, follow DOP-218 — Fraud Referral and Account Compromise.

Contact Center handling steps

  1. Listen for the issue type. Determine whether the customer is reporting:
  2. a billing error,
  3. an unauthorized charge, or
  4. a merchant dispute for goods or services.
  5. Confirm the card product. Verify the customer is disputing a Harborline credit card:
  6. Harborline Cash Rewards Visa
  7. Harborline Travel Visa Signature
  8. Harborline Secured Visa
  9. Collect facts only. Do not investigate merchant activity in the contact center. Document the customer’s statement in the case notes.
  10. Set expectations. Advise that Disputes Operations will review the claim and may request supporting documents.
  11. Route the claim. Submit the case through the standard Contact Center workflow for transfer to Claims Intake.

What to say / what not to say

Say - “I can help start the dispute.” - “A specialist will review the charge and contact you if more information is needed.” - “Keep any receipts, correspondence, or shipping records.”

Do not say - The charge is definitely unauthorized or erroneous. - The merchant will automatically lose the dispute. - The customer will receive a credit immediately. - A specific investigation outcome before review.

Escalate or refer when

Situation Refer to
Customer says the card was lost, stolen, or used without permission Fraud Operations / DOP-218
Customer wants to withdraw the dispute DOP-233 — Customer Withdrawal of a Claim
Customer alleges unfair treatment or dissatisfaction with dispute handling DOP-228 — Escalating Disputes to Complaints
Customer asks about timing, deadlines, or notices DOP-210 / DOP-230

Notes

  • Use this FAQ for Contact Center questions only; it is not a full procedure.
  • If the customer asks for legal rights language, use the approved disclosures and approved scripts only.
  • If the customer presents a complaint about the dispute process itself, follow complaint escalation guidance rather than re-handling the claim in the contact center.
