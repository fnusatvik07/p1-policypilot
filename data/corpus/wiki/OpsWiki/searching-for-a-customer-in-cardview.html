Searching for a customer in CardView

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2023-06-05 · Version 2

WI-003 — Searching for a customer in CardView

Type: Work Instruction
Version: 2
Effective/Issued: 2023-06-05
Status at Issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Applies To: Disputes Operations, Claims Intake, Debit & EFT Investigations, Credit Card Investigations

Purpose

Use this procedure to locate a customer record in CardView when working a dispute or claim in ClaimTrack 2.0. A successful search helps confirm card status, identify the correct account, and open the corresponding servicing record in the Corvane Card Services portal when needed.

This version reflects the ClaimTrack 2.0 release, including automated deadline clocks and updated navigation from the case workspace.

Before you start

  • You must have an open case in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  • Confirm whether the inquiry involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card before searching.
  • Use only the customer information available in the case record or approved supporting documents.
  • If the customer has multiple accounts, search each known identifier separately.
  • Do not rely on nickname or informal data entry alone; use the legal name where available.
  • If the case was opened from a phone or branch channel, confirm the customer identifiers were carried forward into ClaimTrack before searching.

Procedure

  1. Open the case in ClaimTrack 2.0.
    From the ClaimTrack work queue, locate the claim or investigation record and open the case detail screen. If you are in the new Claims Workspace, use the case number to open the record directly.

  2. Review the identifying information.
    Check the customer name, partial account number, card type, and any notes entered by the intake associate. Confirm whether the dispute is tied to a checking account, credit card, or FlexCard record.

  3. Launch CardView from ClaimTrack.
    Select the card servicing option, customer link, or linked account icon from the case detail page. In ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView opens in a separate window or embedded panel, depending on workstation configuration.

  4. Search by the strongest available identifier first.
    Enter one of the following, in this order when available:

  5. full or partial card number,
  6. account number,
  7. customer name and ZIP code,

  8. customer name and date of birth.

  9. Run the search.
    Select Search or press Enter. Review the results list for the closest match. If the first search returns multiple records, refine the search by adding a second identifier.

  10. Verify the correct customer record.
    Confirm that the record matches the case details, including:

  11. legal name,
  12. masked account or card number,
  13. product type,
  14. mailing address,

  15. card status, if displayed.

  16. Open the account summary.
    Select the matching record to open the account summary page. Use this screen to confirm whether the card is active, blocked, expired, or replaced.

  17. If the card data is incomplete, use Corvane Card Services.
    From CardView, select the link to the Corvane portal if card status, issuance history, or network-related details are required. Log in using your assigned credentials and open the customer’s card profile.

  18. Return to ClaimTrack and document the result.
    Record the match result in the case notes, including the identifier used to locate the customer and any relevant card status details. If no match is found, note the search methods attempted.

Tip

If the customer has an account relationship across multiple product types, search each card or account separately. A single customer may have more than one active or closed record in CardView.

Warning

Do not assume the first result is the correct customer. Verify at least two identifiers before using card status information in a dispute or investigation.

Tip

When the search returns many records, narrow the results by adding ZIP code, date of birth, or partial account number rather than repeatedly opening unrelated profiles.

Troubleshooting

  • No results returned: Recheck spacing, punctuation, and spelling in the name field. Try a different identifier from ClaimTrack.
  • Too many results: Add a secondary identifier, such as ZIP code or partial account number.
  • CardView not opening: Confirm the browser session is still active and relaunch from ClaimTrack.
  • Corvane portal unavailable: Continue the case using CardView details and follow local escalation procedures.
