WI-034 — Power of attorney claims checklist

Type: Work Instruction
Status at issue: Active
Effective / issued date: 2023-06-05
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 2

Purpose

Use this checklist when a caller, branch associate, or written submission indicates that a claim is being filed by an attorney-in-fact acting under a power of attorney (POA). This page supports intake and setup only. It does not replace the claim handling standards in CPS-SOP-220 or the dispute-specific procedures for debit, ATM, ACH, credit card, prepaid, or QuickSend claims.

This version reflects the ClaimTrack 2.0 release and the migration to automated deadline clocks. It also aligns terminology with the CPS-SOP series effective 2023-06-05.

Before you start

  • Confirm the customer record and the attorney-in-fact’s identity before discussing account activity.
  • Obtain the POA document or confirm where it is stored in the case file.
  • Do not make a coverage decision at intake.
  • If the representative is acting for a deceased customer, stop and follow CPS-SOP-220 and CPS-SOP-214 as applicable.
  • Use ClaimTrack for the claim record, CardView for card account review, and Corvane Card Services only when card network activity or chargeback indicators must be checked.
  • If the submission originated in a branch, keep the branch scan or ticket with the case record before routing.

Checklist

  1. Open ClaimTrack 2.0.
    From the Claims Intake work queue, search the customer using the account number, last four digits of the card, or existing case number. Confirm you are in the correct consumer file before making any edits.

  2. Verify the representative relationship.
    In the customer profile, open Contacts > Authorized Representatives. Check whether the POA is already listed. If it is not, proceed with intake and flag the case for documentation review.

  3. Create or open the claim case.
    Select New Claim or open the existing case. Enter the product type, channel, transaction date, amount, and dispute reason code. Use the narrative field to note: “Claim initiated by attorney-in-fact under POA.”

  4. Capture POA details in ClaimTrack.
    In Case Notes, record the name of the attorney-in-fact, the date the POA was presented, and the method of receipt (phone, branch, mail, or secure upload). Attach the document image if available. If the image is unreadable, note the defect and continue intake.

  5. Check CardView for account status.
    Launch CardView from the case toolbar. Confirm whether the debit or credit card is active, closed, or restricted. Review recent account activity for obvious duplicate claims, reversals, or merchant credits.

  6. If the claim involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card, confirm processor data in Corvane.
    Open the Corvane Card Services portal and search by card number or token. Review posted and pending items, chargeback history, and any processor-side notes that may affect claim routing. Use this check when the file indicates network activity, card replacement, or a possible processor exception.

  7. Set the claim ownership fields.
    In ClaimTrack, update Customer/Representative fields so the attorney-in-fact is listed as the contact for the case. Leave the customer as the account owner unless system instructions require a different designation.

  8. Apply the correct queue.
    Route the case to the appropriate investigation queue based on product type:

  9. Debit or EFT: Debit & EFT Investigations
  10. Credit card: Credit Card Investigations

  11. Prepaid: Claims Intake for initial review, then route per CPS-SOP-203 and CPS-SOP-208

  12. Add the POA checklist note.
    In the intake summary, confirm that the POA was reviewed for completeness and that the claim was accepted for processing subject to policy review. If the document appears incomplete or unclear, add a follow-up task for the analyst.

  13. Save and submit.
    Save the case in ClaimTrack, verify attachments, and submit to the assigned queue. Confirm that the case status changes to Open – Pending Review and that the automated deadline clock has started.

Tip

If the representative references a branch submission, cross-check the branch ticket or scanned packet with CPS-SOP-224 before routing the case.

Warning

Do not advise the representative that the POA is sufficient to approve a dispute, recover funds, or reverse liability. Intake staff record and route; investigators determine outcome.
