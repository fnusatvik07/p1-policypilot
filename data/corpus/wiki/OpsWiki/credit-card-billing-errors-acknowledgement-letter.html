Credit card billing errors: acknowledgement letter

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2023-06-05 · Version 2

WI-029 — Credit card billing errors: acknowledgement letter

Type: Work instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 2
Effective / issued: 2023-06-05
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this page to prepare and send the acknowledgement letter for a credit card billing error claim after the claim is accepted in ClaimTrack 2.0. The acknowledgement confirms receipt of the dispute, creates the customer correspondence record, and supports downstream investigation activity under CPS-SOP-209 Credit Card Billing Error Disputes and CPS-SOP-230 Customer Letters and Notices.

This version reflects the ClaimTrack 2.0 workflow, including automated deadline clocks and the updated correspondence panel layout.

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim is classified as a Credit Card Billing Error in ClaimTrack 2.0 and the case status is open.
  • Have the customer’s mailing address and the last four digits of the card number available.
  • Verify the merchant name, transaction date, and dispute amount in CardView before generating the letter.
  • If the claim is linked to a chargeback or other card-network activity, review the related record in the Corvane Card Services portal before sending the letter.
  • Use the standard acknowledgement template only. Do not edit approved legal language.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  2. Search by claim number or customer name.
  3. Open the case from the Open Claims queue.

  4. Confirm the claim type reads Credit Card Billing Error.

  5. Review the claim summary.

  6. Select the Claim Details tab.
  7. Check the dispute category, customer contact information, and current status.
  8. Confirm the automated deadline clock is running.

  9. Make sure the claim has not already received an acknowledgement.

  10. Open CardView from the claim workspace.

  11. Select Launch CardView from the ClaimTrack action bar.
  12. If the customer has more than one credit card, choose the card ending in the last four digits shown in ClaimTrack.

  13. Review the most recent statement and the disputed transaction line item.

  14. Verify the billing error information.

  15. Confirm the merchant descriptor, posting date, amount, and statement cycle.
  16. Compare the CardView entry with the claim notes.

  17. If the transaction details do not match, stop and route the case to the assigned analyst for correction before generating the letter.

  18. Open the acknowledgement letter task.

  19. Return to ClaimTrack 2.0.
  20. Select Correspondence.
  21. Choose New Letter and then select Acknowledgement — Credit Card Billing Error.

  22. Use the new correspondence drawer rather than the legacy pop-up window.

  23. Populate the letter fields.

  24. Confirm the customer name and mailing address.
  25. Confirm the account suffix and claim number.
  26. Enter the dispute date, disputed amount, and merchant name exactly as shown in the claim record.

  27. Add any approved internal reference notes in the correspondence comments field only.

  28. Review the Corvane case, if applicable.

  29. Open the related card-services case from the claim links.
  30. Confirm whether a merchant dispute file or network reference number exists.

  31. If a Corvane reference is present, copy the reference number into the case notes; do not place it in the customer letter unless the template allows it.

  32. Generate the letter.

  33. Select Preview.
  34. Read the full letter for spelling, address accuracy, and correct dispute details.
  35. If anything is incorrect, cancel the preview, correct the source field, and regenerate.

  36. Verify the output status changes to Ready to Send before submission.

  37. Send and document the correspondence.

  38. Select Submit to queue the letter for printing and mailing.
  39. Update the ClaimTrack activity log with Acknowledgement letter sent.
  40. Save the case.
  41. If the queue returns an address validation warning, resolve the warning before final submission.

Tip: If the customer address is marked as invalid in ClaimTrack, use the most recent verified mailing address on file before generating the letter.

Warning: Do not send a blank or partially completed acknowledgement. If a required field is missing, stop and correct the record before submission.

Tip: ClaimTrack 2.0 stores the acknowledgment task in the correspondence queue automatically after submission. Review the queue status if the letter does not appear in the activity log within the expected worklist refresh cycle.
