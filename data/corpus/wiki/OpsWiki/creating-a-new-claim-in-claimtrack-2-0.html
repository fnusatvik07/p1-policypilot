WI-002 — Creating a New Claim in ClaimTrack 2.0

Type: Work Instruction
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Effective / Issued Date: 2023-06-05
Status at Issue: Active
Version: 1

Purpose

Use this work instruction to create a new disputes claim in ClaimTrack 2.0 after intake has confirmed that a case should be opened. This page covers the initial case creation workflow in ClaimTrack, with required verification steps in CardView and, when applicable, Corvane Card Services.

This work instruction supports the claim intake and classification procedures in CPS-SOP-201, CPS-SOP-203, and CPS-SOP-234. ClaimTrack 2.0 introduces automated deadline clocks; ensure the claim is opened in the correct channel and product path before proceeding.

Before you start

  • Confirm you have access to ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, and the Corvane portal.
  • Confirm the customer record is already identified and matched to the correct CIF and account.
  • Have the following ready: customer name, account number, transaction date, amount, channel, and dispute reason.
  • If the claim involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card transaction, verify the card status in CardView before creating the case.
  • If the transaction routes through Corvane, be prepared to open the corresponding processor record before saving the claim.

Procedure

  1. Open ClaimTrack 2.0 and sign in using your Harborline network credentials.
    From the landing page, select Claims and then choose New Claim.

  2. In the Customer Search screen, enter the customer’s identifying information.
    Use the most complete available match:

  3. customer name
  4. account number
  5. last four digits of the card or deposit account

  6. phone number, if needed for disambiguation

  7. Select the correct customer record from the results list.
    Review the name, open account indicators, and product relationship before continuing.

  8. In Claim Details, enter the claim source and product type.
    Choose the appropriate channel:

  9. debit card
  10. credit card
  11. prepaid card
  12. ACH
  13. QuickSend

  14. other supported claim type

  15. Open CardView in a separate tab or window and verify the servicing status.
    Confirm the following:

  16. card is active, closed, or restricted
  17. the card last four matches the claim
  18. the account is linked to the correct card product

  19. any recent reissue or replacement status is noted

  20. Return to ClaimTrack 2.0 and complete the transaction information panel.
    Enter:

  21. transaction date
  22. post date, if available
  23. amount
  24. merchant or payee name
  25. dispute reason code

  26. location or terminal indicator, if applicable

  27. If the transaction is a card-network item, open the Corvane portal and locate the matching transaction record.
    Confirm the processor-side details, including:

  28. authorization reference
  29. network transaction identifier
  30. settlement or reversal indicators

  31. any processor notes that affect classification

  32. Back in ClaimTrack 2.0, populate the Claim Classification fields.
    Select the claim category and subcategory consistent with the product and transaction type. If the case requires a specialized path, route it according to the applicable procedure in the CPS-SOP series before saving.

  33. Review the system-generated deadline clocks.
    Confirm the due dates displayed in ClaimTrack 2.0 align with the case type and customer notice workflow. If the clock appears incorrect, stop and escalate through your team lead before finalizing the claim.

  34. Add intake notes in the Case Summary field.
    Document the source of the claim, the verification steps completed, and any exceptions identified during setup. Use concise, factual language.

  35. Attach any available intake documentation.
    Upload supporting screenshots, transaction records, or customer-submitted documents only if permitted by the case path and retention requirements in CPS-SOP-229 and POL-004.

  36. Select Save and Open Case.
    Confirm that ClaimTrack assigns a claim number and that the case appears in your work queue with the correct status.

Tip: If you are unsure whether the item belongs in debit, credit, prepaid, ACH, or QuickSend handling, pause before saving and confirm the path in CPS-SOP-203 and the applicable product procedure.

Warning: Do not create duplicate claims for the same transaction. If you discover an existing open or previously closed claim, follow the duplicate handling process in CPS-SOP-223.

Tip: ClaimTrack 2.0 automatically starts the deadline clock when the case is opened. Accuracy at intake matters; incorrect classification can affect downstream handling.
