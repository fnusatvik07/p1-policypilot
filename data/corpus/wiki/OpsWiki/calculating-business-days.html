WI-011 — Calculating business days

Type: Work instruction
System: OpsWiki
Version: 2
Effective/issued date: 2023-06-05
Status at issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this page to calculate business days for dispute and claims processing dates. Business-day counting is used when setting internal follow-up dates, reviewing deadline status, and checking whether a case is within or outside the applicable timeline. This instruction applies when working in ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, or the Corvane Card Services portal.

For the official holiday list and counting conventions, use CPS-SOP-231 — Business Day Calculation and Holiday Calendar.

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim type and channel in ClaimTrack 2.0 before counting days.
  • Use the Harborline business calendar in CPS-SOP-231; do not rely on a personal calendar.
  • Count only business days, excluding weekends and bank holidays listed in CPS-SOP-231.
  • If a date falls on a non-business day, move the action date to the next business day.
  • For Corvane network activity, verify the transaction date in Corvane before you calculate elapsed time.
  • If the case is already escalated, follow the case notes first and then verify the business-day count.

Procedure

1. Open the case and identify the controlling date

  1. Sign in to ClaimTrack 2.0.
  2. Search for the claim number and open the case record.
  3. Review the case header for:
  4. claim type,
  5. received date,
  6. transaction date,
  7. any prior deadline notes.
  8. Confirm which date controls the count. For most claims, use the date noted in the active work item or the date shown in the claim timeline.
  9. If the claim involves a card transaction, open the related account in CardView and confirm the transaction timestamp.

2. Verify the calendar standard

  1. In ClaimTrack 2.0, open the case and select the Deadlines tab.
  2. Review the automated deadline clock. Confirm whether the system has already populated a due date.
  3. Open CPS-SOP-231 — Business Day Calculation and Holiday Calendar in the Disputes Ops Library.
  4. Confirm the current year’s bank holidays and weekend handling rules.
  5. If the case is tied to a card network event, open the Corvane Card Services portal and verify the network processing date against the claim record.

3. Count business days in ClaimTrack 2.0

  1. In ClaimTrack 2.0, select the applicable date field for the work item.
  2. Choose the date that starts the count.
  3. Use the deadline clock to advance one day at a time and count only days that are not:
  4. Saturday,
  5. Sunday,
  6. a listed holiday in CPS-SOP-231.
  7. Do not count the start date unless the work item or procedure specifically says to include it.
  8. Stop counting when you reach the required business day number.
  9. Save the resulting due date in the case notes or deadline field, as applicable.

4. Confirm the date in CardView

  1. Open CardView for the account.
  2. Review the transaction history and posted date.
  3. Compare the CardView posted date with the claim date in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  4. If the dates differ, use the date required by the active procedure governing the claim type.
  5. Document the selected date in the ClaimTrack notes so the audit trail shows why that date was used.

5. Confirm Corvane dates for network disputes

  1. Open the Corvane Card Services portal for the transaction.
  2. Review the network event date, settlement date, or chargeback-related date shown on the case.
  3. Compare the Corvane date to the ClaimTrack timeline.
  4. If the Corvane date is the controlling date for the work item, use that date for the business-day count.
  5. Save the Corvane reference number in the case notes when available.

6. Record the calculation

  1. Add a short note in ClaimTrack 2.0 showing:
  2. start date,
  3. excluded non-business days,
  4. counted business days,
  5. resulting due date.
  6. If the case has an open task, update the task due date to match the calculation.
  7. If you find a mismatch between systems, notify your lead before sending any customer communication.

Tip

Keep the count visible in the case notes. A clear date trail reduces rework during quality review and helps the next analyst verify the deadline quickly.

Warning

Do not use a calendar app, spreadsheet estimate, or memory-based count for official case processing. Use ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, Corvane, and CPS-SOP-231 together.
