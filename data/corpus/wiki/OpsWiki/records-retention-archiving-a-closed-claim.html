WI-045 — Records retention: archiving a closed claim

Type: Work instruction
Version: 1
Effective/Issued: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this work instruction to archive a closed dispute claim after final disposition is complete. The archive step preserves the claim record, supporting documents, and system references in accordance with DOP-229 — Claim Records and Evidence Retention and the records-retention standards in POL-004 — Records Retention Policy — Consumer Disputes.

This page applies to closed claims managed in ClaimTrack, with customer and account verification completed in CardView and processor evidence retained in Corvane Card Services where applicable.

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim status in ClaimTrack is Closed and the final determination has been issued.
  • Confirm all related customer notices, evidence images, notes, and system comments are saved.
  • If the claim involved a chargeback or processor research, confirm the Corvane case reference is complete.
  • Do not archive an open claim, a claim pending reconsideration, or a claim with missing evidence.
  • If the record is escalated for QA review, follow DOP-227 — Disputes Quality Assurance Review before archiving.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack.
    Search by claim number, card number, or customer name. Open the case detail screen and verify the case header shows Closed.

  2. Review the case summary.
    Confirm the claim type, final outcome, closure date, and assigned team match the final disposition. If the case contains multiple events, review each event tab before proceeding.

  3. Check for active holds or follow-up tasks.
    In the task panel, confirm there are no pending customer callbacks, supervisor reviews, document requests, or unresolved work items. If a task remains open, stop and complete the task first.

  4. Open CardView for account confirmation.
    Launch CardView from the claim screen or open it separately by customer account number. Verify the account status, card status, and last four digits match the claim record.

  5. Validate final account comments.
    In CardView, review the account notes for any late adjustments, dispute reversals, or fee handling entries. If the account note does not reflect the final outcome, return to ClaimTrack and correct the case documentation before archiving.

  6. Open the Corvane Card Services portal, if used.
    If the claim involved debit, credit, or prepaid processing activity that required processor research, open the Corvane portal and locate the matching case or retrieval reference.

  7. Confirm processor records are complete.
    Verify the Corvane file contains the final processor response, chargeback status, and any supporting correspondence. Save the Corvane reference number in the ClaimTrack case notes if it is not already present.

  8. Attach final evidence if needed.
    If the claim file is missing a final notice, image, or processor response, upload the approved document to the claim attachment area before archiving. Use the standard file naming format used by Disputes Operations.

  9. Select the archive action in ClaimTrack.
    From the case actions menu, choose Archive Closed Claim. Review the archive summary carefully, including claim number, closure date, and document count.

  10. Complete the archive confirmation.
    Enter the required confirmation note, then submit the archive action. Wait for the success message before leaving the case.

  11. Verify archived status.
    Reopen the case header and confirm the archive indicator appears. The claim should display as archived and remain searchable for retention and audit purposes.

  12. Record any exceptions.
    If the archive action fails, document the error code or message in the case notes and notify the queue lead. If the record cannot be archived because of missing evidence, route the case to the responsible analyst for correction.

Tip

Archive only after the final version of the claim record is complete. Once archived, do not use the case notes area to add new substantive case content unless the claim is formally reopened.

Warning

Do not delete attachments, overwrite processor references, or store claim evidence outside approved systems. Claim records must remain intact for retention and review under DOP-229.
