Purpose

Use this job aid to answer the most common customer questions about unauthorized-transfer liability for debit cards and unauthorized-use liability for credit cards. For claim handling steps, see DOP-201, DOP-203, DOP-204, DOP-209, and customer notice requirements in DOP-212 and DOP-230. For policy authority, see POL-001, POL-002, and POL-009.

Quick answer table

Product What the customer is asking about Liability limit to state
Harborline Debit Mastercard Loss or theft of debit card; unauthorized transfers If the customer notifies Harborline within 2 business days after learning of the loss or theft of a debit card, the customer's liability for unauthorized transfers is limited to $50.
Harborline Debit Mastercard Loss or theft of debit card; unauthorized transfers If the customer notifies Harborline after those 2 business days, the customer's liability may be up to $500.
Harborline Cash Rewards Visa, Harborline Travel Visa Signature, Harborline Secured Visa Unauthorized use of a credit card A cardholder's liability for unauthorized use of a Harborline credit card cannot exceed $50 under Regulation Z.

What to say

  • Use plain language. Do not argue fault or promise an outcome before the investigation is complete.
  • If the customer is unsure when they learned of the loss or theft, document the best available date and route the claim through the normal intake workflow in ClaimTrack.
  • For debit card disputes, confirm whether the issue involves an unauthorized transfer, card loss, or card theft before quoting a liability limit.
  • For credit card disputes, use the Regulation Z statement above and continue with the billing error workflow in DOP-209.

Decision points

If the issue is about… Route to… Notes
Debit card loss/theft and unauthorized transfers DOP-204 Applies to Harborline Debit Mastercard claims.
ATM withdrawal disputes DOP-205 Liability discussion may still be relevant, but the dispute type controls the workflow.
Credit card unauthorized use DOP-209 Use the Regulation Z cap in customer conversations.
FlexCard prepaid disputes DOP-208 Follow prepaid coverage rules in POL-010 and DOP-208.

Internal reminders

  • Keep the liability discussion separate from timing, provisional credit, and final determination questions.
  • If a customer challenges the limit itself, escalate only if the issue appears to involve a policy interpretation, complaint, or special handling path under DOP-228.
  • Record the explanation in the case notes in ClaimTrack and retain supporting documentation per DOP-229.

FAQ

Q: Do we quote the same limit for every card type?
A: No. Debit and credit card liability statements are handled under different rules and different procedures.

Q: Where do I confirm the customer’s card type?
A: Use CardView for card servicing details and the customer’s claim record in ClaimTrack.

Q: Should I refer the customer to a disclosure?
A: Yes. Use the applicable cardholder agreement or disclosure set for the product involved, including TERMS-DEBIT, TERMS-CASHREWARDS, TERMS-TRAVEL, TERMS-SECURED if applicable, and TERMS-EBD for electronic banking rights.
