WI-043 — QA Reviewer Checklist

Type: Work instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 2
Effective / issued date: 2023-06-05
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this checklist to complete a consistent quality assurance review of dispute claims worked by Claims Intake, Debit & EFT Investigations, Credit Card Investigations, and Chargeback Recovery. This page is intended for Disputes Quality Assurance reviewers using ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, and Corvane Card Services portal access.

A completed review must confirm that the case file supports the claim classification, customer communication, evidence handling, and final disposition documented in the case record. This version reflects the ClaimTrack 2.0 migration and the use of automated deadline clocks.

Before you start

  • Confirm you are assigned the correct review queue in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  • Open the case in read-only mode unless you have been assigned a correction task.
  • Have the applicable procedure open in a second tab, usually CPS-SOP-203, CPS-SOP-210, CPS-SOP-212, or CPS-SOP-227.
  • If the claim includes a card transaction, keep CardView open for account and authorization review.
  • If the claim involves network chargeback activity, keep the Corvane Card Services portal open for supporting entries and merchant-side status.
  • Use the automated deadline clock in ClaimTrack 2.0 as a reference point, but do not rely on it in place of the case record.

QA reviewer checklist

1) Open the assigned review queue

  1. Sign in to ClaimTrack 2.0.
  2. From the home page, select Queues.
  3. Choose Quality Assurance.
  4. Filter by Status = Ready for QA Review.
  5. Sort by Received Date if you are working oldest first.
  6. Open the case number from the queue list.

2) Confirm the case header

  1. Review the Case Summary panel.
  2. Confirm customer name, last four digits of the account or card, claim type, and assigned investigator.
  3. Verify the claim date, received date, and current case status.
  4. Check whether the claim is marked as consumer, business, or joint where applicable.
  5. Confirm the automated deadline clock is displaying the expected milestone dates for the case.

3) Review classification and coverage

  1. Select the Classification tab.
  2. Compare the selected dispute type to the supporting facts in the notes and evidence.
  3. Confirm the investigator used the correct product path:
  4. Debit card POS or ATM disputes
  5. ACH debit disputes
  6. Credit card billing error disputes
  7. Chargeback recovery cases
  8. Open CardView if the review requires account history, card status, or authorization detail.
  9. In CardView, search by account number or card number.
  10. Review recent transactions, card replacement history, and any restriction or closure codes.
  11. Return to ClaimTrack and document whether the classification is supported.

4) Check the investigation timeline

  1. Select the Timeline tab in ClaimTrack.
  2. Review each dated action against the case notes.
  3. Confirm customer contact, investigator actions, merchant follow-up, and customer notice dates are entered in sequence.
  4. Check the timeline against the automated deadline clock to confirm the case is progressing within the expected workflow.
  5. If the case references a card chargeback, open Corvane Card Services.
  6. In Corvane, search using the dispute reference, card number, or merchant ID.
  7. Review chargeback stage, representment response, and any recovery or reversal events.
  8. Compare Corvane activity to the ClaimTrack timeline and note any mismatch.

5) Review evidence and file support

  1. Select the Documents tab.
  2. Confirm the file contains required evidence, notices, and supporting correspondence.
  3. Verify scans are legible and indexed to the correct case.
  4. Check whether any merchant response, affidavit, or customer-submitted documentation is missing.
  5. If supporting documentation is stored outside ClaimTrack, confirm the case notes point to the correct file location.

6) Validate the final disposition

  1. Open the Resolution tab.
  2. Confirm the decision selected by the investigator matches the case facts and supporting records.
  3. Review whether provisional credit, reversal, or final posting activity is accurate.
  4. Check the customer notice status and the final disposition text.
  5. If the case was escalated, confirm the escalation path is documented.

7) Record the QA result

  1. Select QA Review from the case actions menu.
  2. Enter the review outcome.
  3. Add concise comments describing any error, omission, or confirmation of correctness.
  4. If a correction is required, assign the case to the appropriate work queue.
  5. Save the review.
  6. Confirm the case status updates to QA Complete or the assigned follow-up status.

Tip: Keep review comments factual and specific. State what was reviewed, what was found, and what action is needed.

Warning: Do not edit investigator notes unless you are assigned the correction. QA reviewers should document findings, not rewrite the case record.

Tip: If the case includes multiple disputes on the same account, review each claim separately before applying a final QA conclusion.
