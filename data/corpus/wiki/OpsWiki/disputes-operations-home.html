Operational reminders

  • Use CPS-SOP-201 for all intake questions before classifying the claim.
  • Use CPS-SOP-231 when calculating business days or confirming holiday treatment.
  • Use CPS-SOP-214 if a customer requests investigation documents.
  • Use CPS-SOP-229 for evidence and record retention requirements.
  • Use CPS-SOP-234 when a case touches Corvane Card Services.
  • Use CPS-SOP-235 for CFPB portal complaints related to disputes.

Escalation path

For procedural questions, route to the functional manager first. For cross-functional or policy interpretation issues, escalate through the Disputes Operations Steering Group. For control, testing, or regulatory matters, coordinate with Compliance Testing, Regulatory Change Management, or Legal as appropriate.
