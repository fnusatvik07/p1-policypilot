WI-047 — Holiday calendar maintenance

Type: Work instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 2
Effective/issued date: 2023-06-05
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this page to maintain the Holiday Calendar used by Disputes Operations for business-day calculations, deadline monitoring, and claim processing support. This work instruction covers updates in ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, and the Corvane portal. It supports CPS-SOP-231 — Business Day Calculation and Holiday Calendar and is used by Claims Intake, investigations teams, and Quality Assurance.

Before you start

Before you start

  • Confirm the holiday change has been approved through the Operations Training intake queue or the Disputes Operations control mailbox.
  • Verify the effective date before editing any calendar entries.
  • Do not post a holiday if the date is already loaded in more than one system without checking for duplicates.
  • If the update affects a business-day count for open cases, notify the team lead before closing the request.

Required systems

System Use
ClaimTrack 2.0 Primary holiday calendar used for case deadlines
CardView Reference calendar for card servicing screens
Corvane portal Processor calendar for card-related dispute handling

Procedure

1) Update ClaimTrack 2.0

  1. Sign in to ClaimTrack 2.0.
  2. From the home page, select Administration.
  3. Select Calendars.
  4. Open Holiday Calendar.
  5. Select Edit.
  6. Choose the applicable calendar year.
  7. Select Add Holiday.
  8. Enter the holiday name exactly as approved.
  9. Enter the observed date if the office closure is observed on a different business day.
  10. Confirm the system-generated deadline clocks recalculate after the date is added.
  11. Set Status to Active.
  12. Select Save.
  13. Review the calendar grid to confirm the date appears as a non-business day.

Tip: In ClaimTrack 2.0, the deadline clock updates immediately after save. Review the open-case view before leaving the record so you can spot any date that still displays the prior business-day count.

2) Update CardView

  1. Sign in to CardView.
  2. Open the Maintenance menu.
  3. Select Reference Data.
  4. Select Holiday Schedule.
  5. Search for the same calendar year used in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  6. Open the record and select Edit.
  7. Add the holiday date and name.
  8. Confirm the processing indicator is set to exclude the date from standard business-day calculations.
  9. Select Submit.
  10. Refresh the screen and verify the update displays in the schedule summary.

Warning: CardView is used by servicing associates during active customer contact. An incorrect holiday date may affect the expected timing shown on the servicing screen and can cause confusion when a customer asks about next-day follow-up.

3) Update the Corvane portal

  1. Open the Corvane portal.
  2. Sign in with your approved operations credentials.
  3. Select Processor Tools.
  4. Select Calendar Management.
  5. Choose Harborline Disputes from the calendar list.
  6. Select Edit Dates.
  7. Add the holiday date and observed date, if applicable.
  8. Select Validate to run the date check.
  9. Resolve any duplicate-date message before saving.
  10. Select Publish.
  11. Record the confirmation number shown on the success screen.

4) Verify consistency across systems

  1. Return to ClaimTrack 2.0 and confirm the holiday appears in the active year list.
  2. Check CardView to confirm the same date is present.
  3. Reopen the Corvane portal calendar summary and confirm the published status.
  4. Compare the three systems for matching holiday name, date, and observed date.
  5. If all three match, mark the request complete in the maintenance log.

Tips and warnings

Tip: When a holiday falls on a weekend, update the observed closure date in all three systems at the same time. This reduces rework for Claims Intake and the investigations queues.

Warning: Do not remove historical holidays from prior years. Historical dates support case reviews, audit questions, and time-to-response validation.

Tip: If the request comes in after business hours, complete the system update first and send the completion notice the next business day.
