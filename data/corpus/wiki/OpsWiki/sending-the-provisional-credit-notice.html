Sending the provisional credit notice

WI-015 — Sending the provisional credit notice

Type: Work instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective / issued date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this work instruction to send the customer notice when a provisional credit is posted or approved for a dispute case. This page covers the operational sequence in ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane Card Services portal. Use this instruction together with DOP-211 — Provisional Credit and DOP-230 — Customer Letters and Notices.

Before you start

  • Confirm the case is in an eligible provisional credit status in ClaimTrack.
  • Verify the notice type matches the claim channel and product type.
  • Confirm the customer address on file is current before generating any mailed notice.
  • If the provisional credit was processed through Corvane, confirm the posting completed before issuing the notice.
  • If the case has a related fee or overdraft adjustment, review DOP-232 before sending the notice.

Procedure

  1. Open the case in ClaimTrack.
    Search by claim number, customer name, or account number. Open the case from the active work queue.

  2. Confirm provisional credit details.
    Review the case summary panel and verify:

  3. claim type and product
  4. provisional credit amount
  5. posting date
  6. payment method or account to be credited

  7. any case notes from the investigator

  8. Check for required status indicators.
    In the claim timeline, confirm the case reflects a provisional credit action and not a final determination. If the status is unclear, pause and escalate to the assigned investigator.

  9. Open CardView for account validation.
    Select the linked account from ClaimTrack and launch CardView. Confirm the account is open, the last four digits match the claim record, and the credit has posted as expected.

  10. Review Corvane activity if the claim involves a card network adjustment.
    If the case references a debit, credit, or prepaid card network adjustment, open the corresponding record in the Corvane Card Services portal. Confirm the network-side action is complete and the transaction reference is present.

  11. Generate the provisional credit notice.
    Return to ClaimTrack and select Customer Notices from the case actions menu. Choose the provisional credit notice template that corresponds to the case type.

  12. Populate the notice fields.
    Complete all required fields, including:

  13. customer name
  14. mailing address
  15. claim reference number
  16. provisional credit amount
  17. account or card last four digits
  18. notice date

  19. any standard explanatory language shown by the template

  20. Review the notice preview.
    Read the full notice before release. Confirm the amount, date, and case reference are correct. Check that no investigator notes, internal codes, or queue comments appear in the customer-facing text.

  21. Send the notice.
    Select the approved delivery method shown in ClaimTrack. If mail is required, release the notice to print and mail. If the case allows electronic delivery, send the notice through the approved channel shown on the case.

  22. Document completion in ClaimTrack.
    Add a case note stating the notice was sent, the date/time of release, and the delivery method used. Save the case.

  23. Close the task.
    Mark the notice task complete and return the case to the work queue or next processing step, as directed by the case status.

Tip

Use the notice preview screen to verify the customer name and account last four digits before releasing the letter. Most correction work comes from mismatched account selection rather than template errors.

Warning

Do not send a provisional credit notice until the credit action is reflected in the case record. If the notice is sent too early, the customer may receive incomplete or inaccurate information.

Tip

When a case includes a Corvane reference, record the Corvane transaction reference in the claim notes before sending the notice. This helps later review by Quality Assurance and Investigations.
