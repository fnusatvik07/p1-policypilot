Purpose

Use this guide to quickly route and handle disputes under Regulation E (electronic fund transfers and prepaid accounts) versus Regulation Z (credit card billing errors). See POL-001, POL-009, DOP-203, DOP-208, DOP-209, and DOP-210 for detailed procedures.

Fast comparison

Topic Regulation E Regulation Z
Applies to Debit card transactions, ATM, ACH, wires, and FlexCard disputes within coverage scope Credit card billing error disputes
Core workflow Intake, classify, investigate, provisional credit if applicable, final determination Billing error intake, acknowledge, investigate, and resolve
Primary systems ClaimTrack, CardView, HLX Core, Corvane Card Services ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane Card Services
Main team Debit & EFT Investigations; Claims Intake Credit Card Investigations; Claims Intake

Quick rule of thumb

  • If the customer used a Harborline Debit Mastercard, ATM, ACH, or FlexCard, start with Reg E routing.
  • If the customer is disputing a Harborline credit card charge, route under Reg Z.
  • If the customer is unsure, classify the claim first under DOP-203 before discussing liability or deadlines.

Deadline cheat sheet

Reg E - Harborline must investigate and determine whether an error occurred within 10 business days of receiving the notice of error. - If more time is needed, use the extension process in DOP-210 and send any required notice through DOP-230. - Provisional credit handling, when required, follows DOP-211.

Reg Z - Harborline must acknowledge a billing error notice in writing within 30 days of receiving it, unless the dispute is resolved within that time. - Harborline must resolve a billing error within two complete billing cycles, and in no event later than 90 days after receiving the notice.

What associates should look for

Question Reg E indicator Reg Z indicator
Payment type Debit, ACH, ATM, prepaid Credit card
Complaint language Unauthorized transfer, wrong amount, no cash, duplicate EFT Billing error, incorrect charge, goods/services issue
Customer-facing notice Regulation E rights notice Credit card billing error notice

Common processing notes

  • Use ClaimTrack to open the case and record the channel, transaction date, and customer allegation.
  • Verify product type in CardView before coding the dispute.
  • For FlexCard, follow DOP-208 and the coverage guidance in POL-010.
  • For credit card disputes, follow DOP-209 and the billing error workflow in POL-009.
  • If a case crosses categories, escalate to the manager on duty in Disputes Operations for classification review.

Escalate when

  • The product type is unclear.
  • The customer references both debit and credit activity.
  • The deadline is at risk.
  • A complaint or regulator inquiry is received.
