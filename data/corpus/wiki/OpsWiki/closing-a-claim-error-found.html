Closing a claim: error found

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2023-06-05 · Version 2

WI-019 — Closing a Claim: Error Found

Type: Work Instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Effective/Issued Date: 2023-06-05
Status at issue: Active
Version: 2

Purpose

Use this procedure when you are working a dispute claim and identify an error that requires the claim to be closed in ClaimTrack 2.0. This includes cases entered under the wrong claim type, tied to the wrong account, or otherwise unable to continue in the current workstream. ClaimTrack 2.0 includes automated deadline clocks; do not use those clocks as a substitute for confirming the underlying error and documenting the basis for closure.

Before you start

Before you start

  • Confirm you are on the correct customer record and claim number.
  • Review the claim summary, coverage code, and transaction details in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  • Have CardView open for the account status check.
  • If the transaction is a processor item, open Corvane Card Services portal before closing the claim.
  • Do not close a claim until you have confirmed the error and documented the reason in the case notes.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack 2.0.
    From the Claims queue, search by claim number, card number, or customer identifier. Open the claim from the active queue. Confirm the header shows the correct claim ID, product type, and deadline clock.

  2. Review the claim status and work history.
    Select the Timeline tab and review all notes, system events, and assignment changes. Confirm no final determination has already been issued and that the claim is still eligible for closure by error-found handling.

  3. Identify the error that prevents continuation.
    Review the claim facts and determine the root issue. Common examples include:

  4. wrong account selected
  5. duplicate claim already in process
  6. incorrect transaction date or amount
  7. claim opened under the wrong channel or claim type

  8. processor-managed item that requires a different workflow

  9. Verify account and transaction details in CardView.
    Open CardView in a separate window and confirm the account number, card status, and posting details. If the account is closed, restricted, or moved, record that fact in the case notes. If CardView shows a mismatch with the claim data, use the CardView result as the controlling account reference for the closure note.

  10. Check Corvane Card Services if the item is processor-managed.
    For debit, credit, or prepaid card transactions handled through Corvane, open the corresponding transaction inquiry in the Corvane Card Services portal. Confirm whether the item is already matched, reversed, charged back, or pending processor action. Capture the transaction reference if the portal shows an alternate status that explains the error.

  11. Enter a clear closure note in ClaimTrack 2.0.
    Select Notes and add a concise statement that explains the error found and why the claim cannot proceed. Include the source of confirmation, such as CardView or Corvane portal review. Keep the note factual and specific.

  12. Select the correct closure reason.
    In the Close Claim panel, choose the closure reason that best matches the error type. If more than one reason appears applicable, use the one that best reflects the root cause and the next required action. If the reason list includes a processor-specific option, use that option only when Corvane verification supports it.

  13. Close the claim or route it to the proper queue.
    If the claim should not remain open, change the status to Closed and save the record. If the claim must be reassigned instead of closed, route it to the correct queue and leave the claim open only when required by process. Do not leave the claim in an inactive state without a queue owner.

  14. Save the record and confirm the update.
    Refresh the claim to confirm the status change, closure reason, notes, and deadline clock history were saved successfully. If the claim remains in active work after saving, re-open the status field and verify the action was completed.

  15. File any supporting evidence.
    Attach screenshots, transaction details, or Corvane responses if required by the claim record. Follow CPS-SOP-229 — Claim Records and Evidence Retention for storage and indexing of supporting material.

Tips

Tip: If the error is caused by a duplicate claim, check CPS-SOP-223 — Duplicate and Repeat Claims before closing the record. Duplicate claims often require a specific closure reason and note format.

Tip: If the customer should be notified of the closure reason, confirm the notice requirement in CPS-SOP-212 — Final Determination and Customer Notices and CPS-SOP-230 — Customer Letters and Notices before sending any communication.

Warnings

Warning: Do not close a claim based on assumption. The closure reason must match what you verified in ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, or Corvane.

Warning: If the claim appears to involve fraud, account compromise, or unauthorized activity, stop and route to the appropriate process in CPS-SOP-218 — Fraud Referral and Account Compromise.
