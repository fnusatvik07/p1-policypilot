WI-010 — Checking the statement date for timeliness

Type: Work instruction
System: OpsWiki
Version: 1
Effective / issued date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this work instruction to confirm the customer’s statement date and determine whether a dispute is timely before it is classified and routed for investigation. This check is performed early in intake and supports consistent handling across ClaimTrack, CardView, and Corvane Card Services.

This page is used by Claims Intake associates and by any downstream analyst who needs to validate the statement date recorded in the claim file.

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim is already created in ClaimTrack and that the account number is present.
  • Have the customer’s reported transaction date and statement date claim marker available.
  • Use CardView for card-level statement information and Corvane Card Services for processor history when network activity must be confirmed.
  • If the account is not a card account, stop and follow the applicable non-card procedure in the Disputes Ops Library.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack
  2. Sign in to ClaimTrack.
  3. Search for the claim by claim number or customer account number.
  4. Open the claim record.

  5. Review the claim summary panel for the dispute type, transaction date, and any existing statement information.

  6. Locate the statement date in ClaimTrack

  7. Select the Account Details tab.
  8. Review the Statement Date field.
  9. If the field is populated and matches the customer statement cycle, continue to Step 5.

  10. If the field is blank, incomplete, or appears inconsistent, continue to Step 3.

  11. Verify the statement date in CardView

  12. Open CardView in a separate window.
  13. Search for the cardholder using the account number from ClaimTrack.
  14. Select the correct card relationship.
  15. Open the Statements screen.
  16. Identify the most recent statement date that includes the disputed transaction.
  17. Compare the statement date to the date recorded in ClaimTrack.
  18. If CardView and ClaimTrack match, update the claim notes to reflect the verification.

  19. If they do not match, proceed to Step 4.

  20. Confirm activity in Corvane Card Services

  21. Open the Corvane Card Services portal.
  22. Search the account by card number or processor reference.
  23. Open the Transaction History view.
  24. Locate the disputed transaction and any posting or statement-cycle indicators.
  25. Record the processor statement reference in the claim notes.

  26. If Corvane confirms a different cycle date than ClaimTrack or CardView, escalate the discrepancy to the Claims Intake lead before continuing.

  27. Determine timeliness

  28. Compare the verified statement date to the transaction date and the dispute received date.
  29. Use the verified statement date entered in the claim file as the controlling date for timeliness review.
  30. If the statement date supports timely filing, continue to the applicable claim classification procedure.

  31. If the statement date indicates the claim may be outside the allowable window, route the file to the Claims Intake lead for review before customer contact or disposition.

  32. Document the result

  33. In ClaimTrack, add a note stating the source used to verify the statement date.
  34. Include whether the date was confirmed in ClaimTrack, CardView, or Corvane Card Services.
  35. Save the claim record.
  36. Return the claim to the intake queue or the next workflow step.

Tip

Use CardView first when the statement date appears inconsistent in ClaimTrack. In most cases, CardView provides the clearest account-level statement view without requiring a processor lookup.

Warning

Do not rely on a customer’s verbal estimate of the statement date when system records are available. If the system sources conflict, pause processing and escalate the discrepancy.
