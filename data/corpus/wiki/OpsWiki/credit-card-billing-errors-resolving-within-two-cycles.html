Overview

Use this work instruction when handling a credit card billing error dispute in ClaimTrack and coordinating account review, customer contact, and case closure within the standard two-cycle handling path. This page is used with DOP-209 Credit Card Billing Error Disputes, DOP-210 Investigation Timelines and Extensions, DOP-212 Final Determination and Customer Notices, and DOP-230 Customer Letters and Notices.

This page covers the operational sequence from case intake through final notice issuance, including the required checks in ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane Card Services portal.

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim is coded as a credit card billing error and not a debit, ATM, ACH, or fraud case.
  • Open the customer profile in ClaimTrack and verify the card product in CardView.
  • Confirm the open cycle dates using the billing statement available in the dispute record.
  • If the customer already received a merchant credit or submitted a duplicate claim, follow the applicable linked procedure before proceeding.
  • Keep all evidence and notes in ClaimTrack; do not rely on email for case status tracking.

Procedure

  1. Open the case in ClaimTrack
  2. From the Work Queue page, select Credit Card Billing Errors.
  3. Open the oldest open item with status New or Pending Review.
  4. Confirm the dispute reason and amount on the Case Summary tab.

  5. If the case is mislabeled, stop and route it to the correct dispute queue under DOP-203 Claim Classification and Coverage.

  6. Validate the account and card relationship

  7. In ClaimTrack, open the Customer section.
  8. Select Open CardView to launch the card servicing screen.
  9. Verify the cardholder name, account status, and card product.

  10. Confirm the disputed transaction appears on the correct Harborline credit card account.

  11. Review billing cycle timing

  12. In ClaimTrack, open the Timeline tab.
  13. Compare the statement date, transaction date, and dispute receipt date.
  14. Confirm the case is being worked within the two-cycle handling path described in DOP-209 and DOP-210.

  15. If a deadline extension is needed, add the reason in the Case Notes field and follow the extension workflow in DOP-210.

  16. Pull transaction evidence

  17. In CardView, open Transaction Details for the disputed item.
  18. Capture the posting date, authorization information, merchant descriptor, and any prior adjustment.
  19. If merchant data is needed, open the Corvane Card Services portal and search by transaction reference.

  20. Save any response images or supporting records to the ClaimTrack evidence tab.

  21. Check for related credits or prior actions

  22. In ClaimTrack, review the Adjustments and Correspondence tabs.
  23. Confirm whether any merchant credit was received after the claim was opened.
  24. If a credit was received, update the claim record and apply DOP-222 Merchant Credits Received During an Investigation.

  25. Check for repeat filings or duplicate billing error claims under DOP-223 Duplicate and Repeat Claims.

  26. Complete the investigation decision

  27. Record the factual outcome in the Investigation Summary section.
  28. Set the case disposition to Resolved, Denied, or Partial Credit, as applicable.
  29. If provisional credit was issued earlier in the process, confirm the final action matches the disposition and follow DOP-211 Provisional Credit and DOP-213 Reversal of Provisional Credit as needed.

  30. If the case requires customer notice, continue to Step 7.

  31. Issue the final notice

  32. Open the Notices tab in ClaimTrack.
  33. Select the appropriate template under DOP-230 Customer Letters and Notices.
  34. Verify the dollar amount, transaction description, and final determination text.
  35. Send the notice and save the document reference in the case file.

  36. Close the case only after the notice status shows Sent.

  37. Close and archive the record

  38. Confirm all notes are complete and spell-checked.
  39. Attach any supporting Corvane screenshots or statement images.
  40. Set the claim status to Closed.
  41. Ensure the record is retained according to POL-004 Records Retention Policy — Consumer Disputes and DOP-229 Claim Records and Evidence Retention.

Tip

Use the ClaimTrack notes field to document each handoff and evidence check. Clear notes reduce rework when the case is reviewed by Disputes Quality Assurance under DOP-227.

Warning

Do not finalize the case until the notice has been generated and saved. Closing a billing error case without a stored final notice can create an incomplete record.
