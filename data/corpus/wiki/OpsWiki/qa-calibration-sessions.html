WI-044 — QA calibration sessions

Type: Work Instruction
Version: 2
Effective/Issued Date: 2023-06-05
Status at issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Applies to: Disputes Quality Assurance, Claims Intake, Debit & EFT Investigations, Credit Card Investigations, Chargeback Recovery
Change summary: Updated for ClaimTrack 2.0 and the CPS-SOP renumbering.

Purpose

QA calibration sessions align reviewers on issue spotting, coding, and evidence standards before or during active review cycles. Sessions are used to compare independent scoring, resolve interpretation differences, and record decisions for later reference in the Disputes Ops Library.

Before you start

Before you start - Confirm the calibration packet contains at least three completed cases and the current review rubric. - Use only cases already closed in ClaimTrack. - Do not update customer-facing records during calibration. - If the case includes card processor evidence, open Corvane Card Services first. - If the case requires account-level verification, have CardView ready for side-by-side review. - In ClaimTrack 2.0, verify the automated deadline clock is displayed before you begin scoring.

Procedure

  1. Open the calibration queue in ClaimTrack.
    Sign in to ClaimTrack 2.0 and select Queues > QA Calibration. Use the date filter to load the current session. If the queue is empty, refresh once before escalating to the QA lead. Confirm the automated deadline clock appears on the case banner; if it does not, stop and notify the QA lead before proceeding.

  2. Open the first case and review the issue summary.
    From the case list, open the first item. Read the dispute type, product, channel, and prior disposition. Confirm the case is eligible for calibration and not a live production escalation. In the case header, verify the claim is mapped to the correct CPS-SOP procedure family before you score it.

  3. Open CardView for account verification.
    Launch CardView in a separate window. Compare the account number, card status, transaction posting date, and any account notes against the ClaimTrack record. Record any mismatch in the session notes area of ClaimTrack. If CardView and ClaimTrack disagree on account status, pause and confirm whether the discrepancy is due to timing, posting delay, or a corrected account record.

  4. Open Corvane Card Services when network evidence is required.
    If the case involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card transaction handled through Corvane, open the corresponding authorization or chargeback evidence screen. Confirm merchant response codes, reversal indicators, and any network deadlines shown in the file. Use the Corvane transaction reference to cross-check the ClaimTrack event history before you mark evidence complete.

  5. Score the case independently.
    Each reviewer applies the applicable procedure set and records a provisional score. Use the ClaimTrack case notes field to capture the reason for the score, the controlling step, and any evidence relied upon. Do not discuss scores until all reviewers have entered their initial determination. When the case is tied to a specific work instruction, cite the CPS-SOP document ID in the notes field rather than a legacy DOP reference.

  6. Compare scores and reconcile differences.
    After all initial scoring is complete, review each variance line by line. Where reviewers disagree, compare the relevant system record in ClaimTrack, the account details in CardView, and the processor evidence in Corvane. Resolve the difference by citing the procedural step that controls the outcome. If a reviewer’s rationale depends on an older rule interpretation, confirm that the current CPS-SOP version governs before finalizing the discussion.

  7. Document the final calibration decision.
    Enter the final agreed score in ClaimTrack. Update the calibration summary with the rationale, including whether the case was clear, borderline, or required manager review. If the case produced a recurring interpretation issue, flag it for discussion with the QA manager. Ensure the summary is complete enough to support later QA review without reopening the case file.

  8. Save session notes and close the packet.
    Save the final notes in ClaimTrack before closing the case. Save any working files only in the approved Disputes Ops Library location. Do not store calibration files on the shared drive unless the QA lead instructs otherwise. If a packet contains marked-up screenshots or comparator worksheets, label them with the session date and reviewer initials before filing.

Tip: Keep the session focused on the governing procedure, not on individual reviewer preference. When a case turns on evidence quality, cite the record source first and the interpretation second.

Warning: Calibration sessions are for consistency review only. They are not the forum for changing production dispositions, customer letters, or claim ownership.
