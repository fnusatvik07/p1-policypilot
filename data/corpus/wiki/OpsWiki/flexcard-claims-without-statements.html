WI-028 — FlexCard claims without statements

Type: Work instruction
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 2
Effective / issued: 2023-06-05
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this page when a customer reports a FlexCard transaction dispute and does not have a statement to upload or mail. This work instruction covers case creation in ClaimTrack 2.0, balance and transaction review in CardView, and processor review in the Corvane Card Services portal.

This page applies to Claims Intake associates handling FlexCard payroll and general-purpose prepaid claims. For claim classification, deadlines, notice handling, and retention, follow the related CPS-SOP procedures and policy documents listed below.

Before you start

  • Confirm the customer is disputing a FlexCard transaction, not a debit card or credit card item.
  • Confirm the customer does not have a statement available.
  • Gather the transaction date, amount, merchant name, and last four digits of the card.
  • Open ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, and the Corvane Card Services portal in separate tabs.
  • If the customer reports theft, loss, or account compromise, follow the fraud referral path before continuing.
  • If the customer has a receipt, screenshot, or other proof, keep it available for upload.

Procedure

  1. Open ClaimTrack 2.0 and locate the case.
  2. From the Claims Intake queue, click Search Cases.
  3. Enter the customer name, card last four digits, or existing case number.

  4. If no case appears, click New Case.

  5. Set the product and claim details.

  6. In Product, select Prepaid — FlexCard.
  7. In Claim Type, select the closest transaction reason.

  8. Do not select a statement-based evidence type when no statement is available.

  9. Record the statement exception.

  10. In the case notes, enter: Customer does not have a statement available for this claim.
  11. Add the reason the statement is unavailable, if the customer provides one.

  12. Capture whether the customer reviewed online activity, mobile app history, or only the details provided on the call.

  13. Review the card account in CardView.

  14. Click Open CardView from the case navigation panel.
  15. Review card status, available balance, and recent posted activity.

  16. Confirm the disputed transaction appears on the FlexCard account and record the posted date.

  17. Compare the disputed item.

  18. In CardView, locate the merchant name, amount, and transaction date.
  19. Confirm whether the item is pending or posted.

  20. Return to ClaimTrack 2.0 and update the dispute details to match the CardView record.

  21. Check Corvane for processor activity.

  22. Click Open Corvane Portal from ClaimTrack.
  23. Search by card number, merchant, and transaction date.
  24. Review the network record, authorization result, and any reversal or adjustment activity.

  25. If the Corvane record differs from CardView, note both records in the case notes.

  26. Attach available evidence.

  27. Upload any receipt, email, or screenshot the customer provides.
  28. If no statement exists, leave the statement attachment field blank.

  29. Use the case notes to identify which system records were reviewed.

  30. Complete intake and route the case.

  31. Enter the dispute summary, customer contact information, and reviewed transaction data in ClaimTrack 2.0.
  32. Save the case and confirm the status updates to Intake Complete or the local queue equivalent.
  33. Route the case according to the claim type and coverage outcome.
  34. Verify the automated deadline clock is running after save.

Tip: If the merchant description is vague, search CardView first by amount and date, then use Corvane to narrow the record by merchant and authorization time.

Warning: Do not tell the customer to recreate or resend a statement. If no statement exists, proceed using the available account and network records only.

Tip: When the disputed item is still pending, note that status clearly in ClaimTrack 2.0 before routing the case forward.
