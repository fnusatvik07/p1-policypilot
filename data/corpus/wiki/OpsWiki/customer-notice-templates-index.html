Purpose

Use this index to select the correct customer notice template for dispute outcomes, provisional credit actions, and related case correspondence. For procedure details, follow the governing SOPs and policy references listed below.

Template selection guide

Notice type When to use Primary reference
Provisional credit notice When provisional credit is issued or denied under the applicable dispute workflow CPS-SOP-211, CPS-SOP-230
Final determination notice When the investigation is completed and the claim is resolved CPS-SOP-212, CPS-SOP-230
Reversal notice When provisional credit is reversed after review CPS-SOP-213, CPS-SOP-230
Investigation document response When the customer requests copies of documents used in the investigation CPS-SOP-214
Withdrawal acknowledgement When a customer withdraws a claim CPS-SOP-233, CPS-SOP-230

Timing rule

  • Provisional credit notices and final determination notices are mailed to the customer within 3 business days.

Quick FAQ

Q: Which team owns the notice content?
A: Disputes Operations owns the operational notice templates. Changes to customer-facing language must follow policy governance and change management review.

Q: Can associates edit notice text in the case system?
A: No. Use only approved language from CPS-SOP-230 and the current template set. Do not create ad hoc customer letters.

Q: What if the claim involves prepaid, debit, or credit?
A: Use the notice type tied to the resolution event, not the product. Product-specific handling is covered in the relevant dispute SOP.

Q: What is the best reference for business-day counting?
A: Use CPS-SOP-231 for business day calculation and holiday treatment.

Usage notes

  • Confirm the notice type before mailing or uploading the letter to the claim record.
  • For cases with a complaint overlay, coordinate with CPS-SOP-228 before issuance.
  • Retain the mailed notice copy and related case evidence under CPS-SOP-229.
