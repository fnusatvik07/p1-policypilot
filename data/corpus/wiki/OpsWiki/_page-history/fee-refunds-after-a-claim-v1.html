WI-039 — Fee refunds after a claim

Type: Work Instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 1
Effective/issued date: 2017-04-01
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this work instruction when a claim is approved for a refund of fees related to the disputed transaction. This page explains how to verify the fee, process the refund in the applicable system, and document the action in the case record.

This procedure is used by Disputes Operations associates after the claim decision is completed and the fee refund has been confirmed as allowable under the claim outcome. For policy and eligibility rules, see DOP-232 — Fee and Overdraft Refunds Related to Disputes.

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim has a final outcome or approved action that supports a fee refund.
  • Review the claim notes in ClaimTrack for any prior adjustment, merchant credit, or provisional credit activity.
  • Identify the account and product type in CardView or Corvane portal, as applicable.
  • Make sure you have the correct fee name, posting date, and refund amount before you begin.
  • If the fee appears on a deposit account rather than a card account, stop and route the item per the applicable disputes procedure.

Procedure

1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack

  1. Sign in to ClaimTrack.
  2. Search for the claim using the claim number or customer identifier.
  3. Open the case and review the Decision and Notes tabs.
  4. Confirm that the case status supports refund processing.
  5. Verify the fee refund reason is documented in the case notes.

2. Confirm the fee in the servicing system

  1. Open CardView for debit or credit card accounts.
  2. If the account is a prepaid account, open the Corvane portal instead.
  3. Locate the transaction history and identify the disputed fee.
  4. Confirm the fee type, posting date, and amount match the claim record.
  5. If the fee is not visible in the expected account, review the account number and product type before proceeding.

Tip: For card disputes, compare the transaction date in ClaimTrack against the posting date in CardView or Corvane. A mismatch often indicates the wrong account or a duplicate fee.

3. Process the refund

  1. In CardView or Corvane portal, select the refund or adjustment function for the affected account.
  2. Enter the refund amount exactly as approved.
  3. Use the standard refund description for disputed-fee adjustments.
  4. Submit the transaction.
  5. Record the system confirmation number in ClaimTrack notes.

If the system requires a reason code, use the disputes-related fee refund code approved for your queue. Do not use a general courtesy refund code unless the claim outcome specifically instructs you to do so.

Warning: Do not combine multiple claim-related fees into a single refund unless the claim record and applicable work instruction specifically permit a combined adjustment. Each refund must be traceable to the approved claim outcome.

4. Document the case

  1. Return to ClaimTrack.
  2. Add a note that includes:
  3. fee name
  4. refund amount
  5. refund date
  6. system used to process the refund
  7. confirmation number
  8. Update the case outcome or task status to show that the fee refund has been completed.
  9. Attach any supporting screen prints only if required by the claim queue standard.
  10. Close the task.

5. Verify completion

  1. Reopen the claim summary in ClaimTrack.
  2. Confirm the note is saved and the task status reflects completion.
  3. If the refund was processed in error, follow the adjustment correction process used by your queue and notify the lead.

Tip: If the customer also received provisional credit, check whether the fee refund affects the final claim summary before closing the case.
