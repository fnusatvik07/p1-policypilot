Purpose

Use this FAQ when taking calls about Harborline Debit Mastercard transaction disputes. This page supports front-line handling in Genesys and sets the standard for routing, basic intake, and customer expectations. For full processing steps, see DOP-225 — Claims Received by the Contact Center and DOP-201 — Claim Intake (All Channels).

Quick routing guide

Customer issue What to do Reference
Card not present, card lost/stolen, or account takeover Route as potential fraud and follow fraud escalation path DOP-218
ATM withdrawal dispute Use the ATM dispute path DOP-205
Point-of-sale purchase dispute Use the debit card POS dispute path DOP-204
Merchant says “refund issued” but customer has not received it Capture details and route for investigation DOP-222
Customer wants to cancel a filed claim Document withdrawal request and route per procedure DOP-233

What to collect on the call

Before ending the call, confirm and document:

  • Customer name and preferred callback number
  • Last 4 digits of the debit card
  • Transaction date, amount, merchant name, and location
  • Whether the card is in the customer’s possession
  • Whether the customer already contacted the merchant
  • Whether the customer has supporting documents or a receipt

If the customer is unable to provide full details, record what is available and continue with the claim intake steps in DOP-201.

Common customer questions

Question Approved response
“Can you tell me right now if I’ll get my money back?” Explain that the dispute must be reviewed first and that final determination depends on the investigation.
“Do I need to contact the merchant?” Advise the customer that merchant contact is often helpful and may be required depending on the claim type.
“Will my card still work?” If the card is not compromised, normal usage may continue; if compromise is suspected, follow fraud escalation procedures.
“How long will this take?” Advise that timing depends on claim type and investigation steps; do not promise a result date outside the published process.

When to escalate

Escalate to a supervisor or follow the applicable referral path when:

  • The customer alleges unauthorized activity and the card may be compromised
  • The customer is disputing multiple transactions from different merchants
  • The customer is requesting legal action, external complaint handling, or media contact
  • The customer is a vulnerable or high-risk caller requiring extra support
  • The issue appears to involve a duplicate or repeat claim

For complaints, use DOP-228 — Escalating Disputes to Complaints.

Agent reminders

  • Do not promise provisional credit unless the claim qualifies and the investigation path supports it.
  • Do not advise the customer to file a second claim for the same transaction.
  • Use clear language: “dispute,” “claim,” and “investigation” rather than “guaranteed refund.”
  • Check CardView for card status and recent activity before submitting the claim.
  • Create the case in ClaimTrack only after intake is complete.
