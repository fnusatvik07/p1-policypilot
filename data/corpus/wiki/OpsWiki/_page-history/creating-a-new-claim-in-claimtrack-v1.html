WI-001 — Creating a New Claim in ClaimTrack

Type: Work Instruction
Version: 1
Effective / Issued Date: 2017-04-01
Status at Issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this work instruction to open a new consumer dispute or fraud claim in ClaimTrack after intake is complete and the claim has been categorized for handling. This page covers the initial case setup only. For claim classification, timelines, notices, and investigation handling, see the related DOP procedures listed below.

Before you start

  • Confirm you have the customer name, account number, contact details, and the transaction date(s) reported.
  • Confirm the claim type and channel source from the intake record.
  • Have access to ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane Card Services portal.
  • If the customer is reporting a debit, credit, or prepaid card issue, verify the card relationship in CardView before creating the case.
  • Do not create duplicate cases for the same transaction set. Check for prior claims first.

Procedure

  1. Open ClaimTrack.
    Sign in and select Claims from the main navigation menu.

  2. Start a new case.
    Click New Claim. The Create Claim screen opens.

  3. Search for the customer.
    Enter the customer’s last name and account number in the search fields, then click Search.

  4. Review the matched customer profile.
    Select the correct customer from the results list. Confirm the address, phone number, and account relationship shown in the profile header.

  5. Open CardView.
    From ClaimTrack, click Open CardView or launch CardView in a second window if required by your workstation setup.

  6. Verify the card and account status.
    In CardView, enter the card number or masked account number and review:

  7. card status
  8. product type
  9. last four digits
  10. current account standing

Return to ClaimTrack after confirming the account details.

  1. Return to the Create Claim screen.
    Select the claim category from the drop-down list. Use the category that matches the customer’s report and the intake notes.

  2. Enter the transaction details.
    Populate the transaction date, posted date if available, amount, merchant name, and transaction description. Enter each disputed item on a separate line where applicable.

  3. Add the intake source and summary.
    In the comments field, enter the intake channel, a brief summary of the issue, and any immediate customer statements that affect case setup.

  4. Check for prior activity.
    Before saving, search ClaimTrack for prior claims tied to the same customer or transaction. If a prior claim exists, review the case history and determine whether this submission should be linked or treated as a repeat filing.

  5. Open the Corvane Card Services portal if network processing is involved.
    For debit, credit, or prepaid card items processed through Corvane, open the portal and verify whether the transaction already appears in a prior adjustment, reversal, or chargeback file.

  6. Document the Corvane check.
    Record the portal review outcome in the ClaimTrack notes. If the transaction shows prior network activity, note the reference number and status exactly as displayed.

  7. Save the claim.
    Click Save. ClaimTrack assigns the case number and opens the case summary view.

  8. Confirm the case opened correctly.
    Review the case summary for:

    • customer name
    • account number
    • claim category
    • transaction count
    • case number

  9. Route the claim according to workflow.
    Leave the case in the assigned intake queue unless a supervisor or procedure requires immediate reassignment.

Tip

Use the ClaimTrack notes field to record only concise setup facts. Save investigative detail for the assigned analyst workflow.

Warning

If the customer information in ClaimTrack does not match CardView, stop and escalate through the intake lead before saving the case. Do not proceed with mismatched identity or account data.
