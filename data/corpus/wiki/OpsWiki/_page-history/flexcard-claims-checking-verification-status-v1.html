Purpose

Use this page to confirm whether a FlexCard claim has been verified in the operational systems used by Claims Intake and downstream dispute teams. Verification status is checked in ClaimTrack, CardView, and, when applicable, the Corvane Card Services portal. This work instruction is used after a claim is created and before any further action is taken in the dispute workflow.

Before you start

  • Confirm you have the claim number, customer name, and last four digits of the FlexCard.
  • Have access to ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane portal.
  • Use the current claim record only; do not rely on screenshots or notes from prior cases unless they are attached to the same claim.
  • If the customer has reported a payroll or general-purpose FlexCard, confirm you are reviewing the correct card profile before proceeding.

Procedure

1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack

  1. Sign in to ClaimTrack.
  2. Search for the claim using the claim number. If the claim number is unavailable, search by customer name and last four digits.
  3. Open the claim record.
  4. Review the claim summary panel and locate the verification status field.

The claim summary will typically display one of the following values:

Status shown Meaning
Verified Claim verification is complete in the dispute file
Pending Verification has not yet been completed
Returned The claim was sent back for correction or additional review

2. Confirm the card details in CardView

  1. From the ClaimTrack record, open the linked card profile in CardView.
  2. Confirm the card number suffix matches the claim record.
  3. Review the card status, program type, and issue date.
  4. Check for any flags or servicing notes that would explain a delay in verification.

If the card profile does not match the claim record, stop and correct the case identity before making any status update.

Tip

A mismatch between ClaimTrack and CardView usually means the claim was opened against the wrong card profile or the customer has multiple FlexCard accounts. Verify the card suffix before you continue.

3. Check the Corvane Card Services portal

  1. In ClaimTrack, select the Processor Status or External Activity link, if available.
  2. Launch the Corvane Card Services portal.
  3. Search by card number suffix, claim number, or case reference, depending on the portal view.
  4. Review the latest processor activity for verification-related entries.

Look for entries showing that the claim was: - accepted into the processor queue, - returned for missing data, - marked complete, or - held for review.

If the Corvane portal shows a different status than ClaimTrack, follow the case record in ClaimTrack and escalate the discrepancy through the normal queue handoff process.

4. Update the case record

  1. Return to ClaimTrack.
  2. Enter a concise note summarizing the verification result and the system checked.
  3. Include the date, time, and your associate ID.
  4. Save the case record.

Use objective language only. Example:

  • “Verified in ClaimTrack and CardView; Corvane portal shows completed processor activity.”
  • “Pending in ClaimTrack; CardView matches; Corvane portal shows queue hold for review.”

Warning

Do not overwrite system-generated verification fields. Add a note instead of editing processor-owned data unless you are specifically authorized to correct the record.

5. Route unresolved cases

  1. If the verification status remains Pending after all system checks, leave the claim in its current workflow status.
  2. Send the case to the appropriate queue according to the claim type and routing instructions in the claim record.
  3. If the status discrepancy affects claim handling, notify the designated lead through the standard team channel.
