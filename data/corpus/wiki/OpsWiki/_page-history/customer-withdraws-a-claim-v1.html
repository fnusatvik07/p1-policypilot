Customer withdraws a claim

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2017-04-01 · Version 1

WI-038 — Customer withdraws a claim

Type: Work instruction
System scope: ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective/issued date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this work instruction when a customer tells Claims Intake that they want to withdraw a previously opened dispute or claim. A withdrawal ends further investigation activity only after the request has been verified, documented, and processed in the case record. If the claim has been referred to another team or processor, complete the withdrawal in the case system first and then update any downstream status as required by the case notes.

Before you start

Before you start

  • Confirm you are working the correct customer and the correct claim number.
  • Confirm the customer is authorized to withdraw the claim.
  • Check whether a provisional credit was already issued.
  • Review the claim type and current status in ClaimTrack before making any changes.
  • If the claim has already been sent to Corvane Card Services, complete the Corvane update after the ClaimTrack status change.
  • Do not close or void supporting notes until the withdrawal is fully documented.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack.
  2. Sign in to ClaimTrack.
  3. From the home page, select Search Claims.
  4. Enter the claim number or customer identifier in the search field.

  5. Open the matching claim from the results list.

  6. Verify the withdrawal request.

  7. Review the claim summary panel for the current status.
  8. Open the Notes tab and confirm there is an entry showing the customer requested withdrawal.

  9. If the request was received by phone or branch, verify the channel and the associate ID in the note.

  10. Update the claim status in ClaimTrack.

  11. Select Actions.
  12. Choose Withdraw Claim.
  13. In the withdrawal dialog, select the appropriate reason code for customer request.
  14. Enter a concise note stating that the customer requested withdrawal and the date the request was received.

  15. Select Save.

  16. Review provisional credit status.

  17. In the claim header, check whether provisional credit was issued.
  18. If the claim shows provisional credit, add a note that the withdrawal may require follow-up under the applicable recovery process.

  19. Do not manually reverse funds from the claim screen.

  20. Update CardView if the claim is card-related.

  21. Open CardView in a new window.
  22. Search for the cardholder using the account number or card number.
  23. Open the Disputes section.
  24. Confirm the claim status now shows Withdrawn or the equivalent closed-withdrawal indicator.

  25. Add a short servicing note only if the card record does not already reflect the withdrawal.

  26. Update Corvane Card Services if the case was sent downstream.

  27. Open the Corvane Card Services portal.
  28. Locate the claim using the claim reference or card account.
  29. If the case is in an open chargeback or processing status, select the matching case record.
  30. Change the case disposition to Customer withdrew claim.

  31. Save the update and confirm the case reflects the withdrawn status.

  32. Complete the case documentation.

  33. Return to ClaimTrack.
  34. Add a final note summarizing:
    • customer request,
    • date received,
    • systems updated,
    • whether provisional credit was present,
    • whether Corvane was updated.
  35. Attach any supporting documentation already received, if applicable.

  36. Leave the claim in the system status that corresponds to a customer withdrawal.

  37. Close the work item.

  38. Confirm the case appears in your completed queue or worklist.
  39. If the withdrawal created a follow-up task, route it to the appropriate queue before closing your work.
  40. Verify the case is no longer active in your open queue.

Tip: If the customer changes their mind after withdrawal, a new claim must be handled as a new request and evaluated using the normal intake process.

Warning: Never withdraw a claim based on a third party’s request unless the customer authorization on file clearly permits that action.

Tip: Keep notes short, factual, and dated. The case record should show exactly who requested the withdrawal and when it was processed.
