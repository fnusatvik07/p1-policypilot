WI-024 — Foreign-initiated transfer identification

Document type: Work Instruction
Version: 1
Effective date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this procedure to identify whether a disputed or reported transfer was initiated outside the United States and to document that determination in ClaimTrack, CardView, and Corvane Card Services records. This work instruction supports claim classification, evidence handling, and downstream investigation routing under the dispute procedures referenced in DOP-201, DOP-203, DOP-206, and DOP-234.

Before you start

Before you start: Confirm that the claim is open in ClaimTrack and that you have the card/account number, transaction date, amount, and merchant or transfer descriptor from the intake record. If the transaction is a card-not-present or card-present debit item processed through Corvane, use the Corvane portal to verify network data before making a final foreign-origin determination.

You will use:

  • ClaimTrack for case notes, disposition coding, and workflow status
  • CardView for cardholder account and transaction detail
  • Corvane Card Services portal for processor and network indicators

If the item is already classified under DOP-204, DOP-205, or DOP-217, follow the primary procedure first and use this work instruction only to complete the foreign-initiated check.

Procedure

  1. Open the case in ClaimTrack.
    From the ClaimTrack work queue, open the customer claim and review the intake summary. Confirm the transaction line item is the one under review.

  2. Review the transaction details in ClaimTrack.
    Select the transaction and check for any of the following:

  3. foreign country code
  4. international merchant location
  5. overseas IP or terminal indicator
  6. non-U.S. authorization or settlement marker

  7. foreign currency conversion reference

  8. Cross-check the card record in CardView.
    Launch CardView from the ClaimTrack case tools menu. On the card servicing screen, review:

  9. authorization location
  10. merchant country, if displayed
  11. card presentment details

  12. reversal or adjustment activity tied to the same item

  13. Open the Corvane Card Services portal.
    From the case tools panel, select Corvane Portal and search by card number, transaction date, and amount. Open the network transaction record.

  14. Confirm foreign-origin indicators in Corvane.
    In the transaction record, review:

  15. merchant country
  16. acquirer country
  17. terminal country
  18. ISO message fields, when populated

  19. interchange or network notes describing international acceptance

  20. Determine whether the transfer was foreign-initiated.
    Mark the item as foreign-initiated if the processor or network record shows initiation outside the United States, even if the merchant descriptor includes a U.S. billing name. If the country indicators conflict, use the most specific network field available and escalate for review rather than guessing.

  21. Update ClaimTrack.
    In the case activity notes, record:

  22. the systems reviewed
  23. the foreign indicator(s) found
  24. the date and time of review

  25. your conclusion: foreign-initiated, not foreign-initiated, or inconclusive

  26. Apply the appropriate case flag.
    In ClaimTrack, set the case flag for foreign-initiated transfer identification. If the item requires further handling under another dispute procedure, route the case to the appropriate queue and note the basis for transfer.

  27. Save and close the record review.
    Save all notes, return the case to the work queue, and ensure the status reflects the next required action.

Tip: When the descriptor is abbreviated or unfamiliar, use Corvane before relying on merchant text alone. Merchant text may reflect the processor’s billing label rather than the actual point of initiation.

Warning: Do not use customer statements alone to determine foreign origin. Documented network or processor data must support the conclusion.
