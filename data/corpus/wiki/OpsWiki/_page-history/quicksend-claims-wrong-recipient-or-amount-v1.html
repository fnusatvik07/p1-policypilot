WI-026 — QuickSend claims: wrong recipient or amount

Type: Work Instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 1
Effective/issued date: 2020-05-01
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this procedure when a customer claims a Harborline QuickSend transfer was sent to the wrong recipient or for the wrong amount. This work instruction covers intake, system verification, claim setup, and handoff for investigation under DOP-207 — QuickSend Person-to-Person Payment Disputes.

[!IMPORTANT] If the customer is reporting suspected account takeover, unauthorized access, or a broader fraud pattern, stop this workflow and follow DOP-218 — Fraud Referral and Account Compromise.

Before you start

[!NOTE] Confirm the transfer type before opening a claim.

This procedure applies only to Harborline QuickSend transfers initiated in the mobile app under the QuickSend service terms. If the payment was made through external ACH, wire, debit card, or bill pay, use the applicable procedure instead.

Have these items ready: - Customer name and account number - Transfer date, amount, and recipient details - Caller identity verification or authenticated digital session record - Any customer statement about whether the transfer was sent to the wrong person or for the wrong amount

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack.
  2. Sign in to ClaimTrack.
  3. Select New Claim.
  4. In the product list, choose QuickSend.
  5. In the issue type field, select Wrong recipient / wrong amount.
  6. Enter the customer’s account number and the transfer date.

  7. Save the draft claim to generate the claim number.

  8. Verify the transfer details in CardView and HLX Core.

  9. Open CardView and search the customer profile using the debit account linked to the QuickSend transfer.
  10. Review the QuickSend transaction reference number, sender funding account, and posted amount.
  11. Open HLX Core and confirm that the debit posted to the correct deposit account.
  12. Compare the posted amount with the customer’s statement of the error.

  13. If the transfer was posted to the wrong deposit account, note the account number and continue.

  14. Review the QuickSend record in the Corvane portal.

  15. Launch the Corvane Card Services portal.
  16. Search the transaction by the QuickSend reference number.
  17. Review recipient identifier, transfer status, timestamp, and any reversal or return activity.
  18. Capture the recipient name exactly as shown in the portal.

  19. If the recipient data differs from the customer’s intended recipient, document the variance in ClaimTrack notes.

  20. Classify the claim in ClaimTrack.

  21. Open the Classification tab.
  22. Set the claim reason to Wrong recipient or Wrong amount, as appropriate.
  23. Add the customer’s description in the narrative field.
  24. Attach any available screenshots or transaction notes from CardView and the Corvane portal.

  25. Mark the claim as QuickSend investigation required.

  26. Check for duplicate or related activity.

  27. Search ClaimTrack for prior QuickSend claims on the same account.
  28. Review whether the customer has already disputed the same transfer.
  29. If a duplicate exists, follow DOP-223 — Duplicate and Repeat Claims.

  30. If the claim includes charges, reversals, or follow-up fee issues, route accordingly under DOP-232 — Fee and Overdraft Refunds Related to Disputes.

  31. Route the claim for investigation.

  32. Assign the claim to the QuickSend queue in Claims Intake.
  33. Set the investigation reason code in ClaimTrack.
  34. Add a note stating whether the issue is recipient mismatch, amount mismatch, or both.
  35. If the customer requests written confirmation, follow DOP-202 — Oral Notices and Written Confirmation.
  36. If the customer asks for documents later, see DOP-214 — Customer Requests for Investigation Documents.

[!TIP] In Corvane, always copy the recipient identifier exactly as displayed. Do not normalize nicknames or abbreviations. Small differences can affect the investigation outcome.

[!WARNING] Do not promise a refund or reversal at intake. Final disposition depends on the investigation results and the applicable QuickSend terms.
