WI-044 — QA calibration sessions

Type: Work Instruction
Version: 1
Effective/Issued Date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Applies to: Disputes Quality Assurance, Claims Intake, Debit & EFT Investigations, Credit Card Investigations, Chargeback Recovery

Purpose

QA calibration sessions align reviewers on issue spotting, coding, and evidence standards before or during active review cycles. Sessions are used to compare independent scoring, resolve interpretation differences, and record decisions for later reference in Disputes Ops Library.

Before you start

Before you start - Confirm the calibration packet contains at least three completed cases and the current review rubric. - Use only cases already closed in ClaimTrack. - Do not update customer-facing records during calibration. - If the case includes card processor evidence, open Corvane Card Services first. - If the case requires account-level verification, have CardView ready for side-by-side review.

Procedure

  1. Open the calibration queue in ClaimTrack.
    Sign in to ClaimTrack and select Queues > QA Calibration. Use the date filter to load the current session. If the queue is empty, refresh once before escalating to the QA lead.

  2. Open the first case and review the issue summary.
    From the case list, open the first item. Read the dispute type, product, channel, and prior disposition. Confirm the case is eligible for calibration and not a live production escalation.

  3. Open CardView for account verification.
    Launch CardView in a separate window. Compare the account number, card status, transaction posting date, and any account notes against the ClaimTrack record. Record any mismatch in the session notes area of ClaimTrack.

  4. Open Corvane Card Services when network evidence is required.
    If the case involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card transaction handled through Corvane, open the corresponding authorization or chargeback evidence screen. Confirm merchant response codes, reversal indicators, and any network deadlines shown in the file.

  5. Score the case independently.
    Each reviewer applies the applicable procedure set and records a provisional score. Use the ClaimTrack case notes field to capture the reason for the score, the controlling step, and any evidence relied upon. Do not discuss scores until all reviewers have entered their initial determination.

  6. Compare scores and reconcile differences.
    After all initial scoring is complete, review each variance line by line. Where reviewers disagree, compare the relevant system record in ClaimTrack, the account details in CardView, and the processor evidence in Corvane. Resolve the difference by citing the procedural step that controls the outcome.

  7. Document the final calibration decision.
    Enter the final agreed score in ClaimTrack. Update the calibration summary with the rationale, including whether the case was clear, borderline, or required manager review. If the case produced a recurring interpretation issue, flag it for discussion with the QA manager.

  8. Save session notes and close the packet.
    Save the final notes in ClaimTrack before closing the case. Save any working files only in the approved Disputes Ops Library location. Do not store calibration files on the shared drive unless the QA lead instructs otherwise.

Tip: Keep the session focused on the governing procedure, not on individual reviewer preference. When a case turns on evidence quality, cite the record source first and the interpretation second.

Warning: Calibration sessions are for consistency review only. They are not the forum for changing production dispositions, customer letters, or claim ownership.
