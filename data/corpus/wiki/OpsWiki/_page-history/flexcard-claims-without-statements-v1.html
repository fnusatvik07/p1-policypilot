WI-028 — FlexCard claims without statements

Type: Work instruction
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective / issued: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this procedure when a customer reports a FlexCard transaction dispute and does not have a statement to upload or mail. This work instruction covers the intake and case set-up steps in ClaimTrack, the balance/transaction verification in CardView, and the network file review in the Corvane Card Services portal.

This page is used by Claims Intake associates and applies to both FlexCard payroll and general-purpose prepaid claims. For claim classification, investigation timelines, notices, and retention requirements, follow the related DOP procedures and policy documents listed below.

Before you start

  • Confirm the customer is calling about a FlexCard transaction and not a debit or credit card.
  • Confirm the customer does not have a statement available.
  • Gather the transaction date, amount, merchant name, and last four digits of the card.
  • Open ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane portal in separate tabs before starting the case.
  • If the customer indicates theft, loss, or account compromise, follow the appropriate fraud referral path before continuing.

Procedure

  1. Open ClaimTrack and search for the customer.
  2. From the Claims Intake work queue, click Search Cases.
  3. Enter the customer name, card last four digits, or case number.

  4. If no case exists, click New Case.

  5. Set the product and claim type.

  6. In the Product field, select Prepaid — FlexCard.
  7. In the Claim Type field, select the closest transaction reason.

  8. If the customer has no statement, do not select a statement-based evidence type.

  9. Document the statement exception.

  10. In the case notes, enter: Customer does not have a statement available for this claim.
  11. Capture the reason the statement is unavailable, if provided.

  12. Note whether the customer has access to online activity or only the transaction details provided during the call.

  13. Verify card status in CardView.

  14. Click Open CardView from the case navigation panel.
  15. Review the card status, available balance, and recent posted activity.

  16. Confirm the disputed transaction appears on the FlexCard account and record the posted date.

  17. Compare the transaction details.

  18. In CardView, locate the disputed merchant and amount.
  19. Confirm whether the transaction is pending or posted.

  20. Return to ClaimTrack and update the dispute details to match the CardView record.

  21. Review Corvane for network and processor activity.

  22. Click Open Corvane Portal from ClaimTrack.
  23. Search by card number, merchant, and transaction date.

  24. Review the network record, authorization result, and any reversal or adjustment activity.

  25. Attach available evidence.

  26. If the customer provides a receipt, email, or screenshot, upload it to ClaimTrack.
  27. If no statement exists, leave the statement attachment field blank and continue with the available records.

  28. Use the case notes to identify which system records were reviewed.

  29. Complete the intake summary.

  30. In ClaimTrack, enter the dispute summary, customer contact information, and all reviewed transaction data.
  31. Save the case and verify that the status changes to Intake Complete or the local queue equivalent.
  32. Route the case according to the claim type and coverage outcome.

Tip: If the customer gives only a vague merchant description, search CardView first by amount and date, then use Corvane to narrow the record by merchant and authorization time.

Warning: Do not tell the customer to recreate or resend a statement. If no statement exists, proceed using the available account and network records only.

Tip: When the disputed item is still pending, note that status clearly in ClaimTrack before routing the case forward.
