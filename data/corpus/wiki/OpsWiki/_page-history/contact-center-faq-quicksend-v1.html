Purpose

Use this job aid when assisting customers with Harborline QuickSend in the mobile app. QuickSend is a person-to-person transfer service and is handled differently from debit card, ACH, and credit card disputes. See DOP-207 — QuickSend Person-to-Person Payment Disputes and TERMS-QS — Harborline QuickSend Service Terms.

Quick answers

Question Answer
Can a customer dispute a QuickSend transfer they sent? QuickSend payments initiated by the customer are final and are not eligible for dispute.
What if the customer says the transfer was sent to the wrong person? Explain that QuickSend transfers initiated by the customer are final. Do not open a dispute case for the transfer itself.
What if the customer believes someone accessed their app and sent a QuickSend transfer without permission? Follow the fraud and unauthorized transfer process in DOP-218 — Fraud Referral and Account Compromise and POL-002 — Unauthorized Transfer Liability Policy.
Does QuickSend follow Regulation E? Refer to TERMS-QS and the applicable consumer transfer policies. If the issue involves unauthorized access or account compromise, use the standard intake and classification process.
Which system should I use? Use the normal contact center workflow and document the interaction in the applicable case or referral channel.

Handling steps for associates

  1. Confirm the product. Verify the customer is asking about QuickSend, not a debit card transaction, ACH debit, wire transfer, or bill payment.
  2. Clarify the issue. Determine whether the customer is disputing:
  3. a transfer they authorized,
  4. a mistaken payment to the wrong recipient, or
  5. a potentially unauthorized transfer.
  6. Set expectations. If the customer initiated the transfer, advise that QuickSend transfers are final.
  7. Escalate only when appropriate. If the customer alleges compromise, fraud, identity theft, or device access issues, route per DOP-218.
  8. Do not promise reversal. Do not advise that QuickSend funds can be recalled through a dispute process.

Common call language

  • “I can review the account activity with you.”
  • “QuickSend transfers you authorize are final.”
  • “If you believe your app or account was accessed without permission, I can help start the fraud review path.”

Do not

  • Open a disputes claim for a customer-approved QuickSend transfer.
  • Classify QuickSend as a debit card purchase, ACH debit, or credit card billing error.
  • Advise that QuickSend has chargeback rights.
