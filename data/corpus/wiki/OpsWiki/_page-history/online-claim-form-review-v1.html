WI-042 — Online Claim Form Review

Type: Work instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective/Issued Date: 2017-04-01
Status at Issue: Active

Purpose

Use this page to review an online claim form submitted through the consumer dispute intake workflow. This review confirms that the claim is readable, complete enough to open, and routed to the correct dispute type before the case is worked in ClaimTrack.

This instruction applies to claims entered from online channels and reviewed by Claims Intake associates. For classification, notice, timeline, and investigation standards, follow the applicable DOP procedures and product policies listed in Related pages.

Before you start

  • Confirm you are logged into ClaimTrack and CardView with your assigned network credentials.
  • Have the customer’s name, last four digits of the account or card number, and the submitted online form reference available.
  • If the claim involves a debit or credit card transaction, CardView must be open before you begin review.
  • If the claim includes a merchant, transaction amount, or network reference that is not clear from the form, use Corvane Card Services only to validate the transaction details.
  • Do not change the customer’s statement language unless you are correcting a clear data-entry error.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim queue in ClaimTrack.
    From the ClaimTrack home page, select Work Queue and then choose Online Claims Review. Refresh the queue if it does not display current items.

  2. Open the next claim form.
    Select the oldest unreviewed item at the top of the queue. Open the record and confirm the case status is New or Pending Review.

  3. Verify customer and account details.
    Compare the name, contact information, and account or card number on the form with the customer record in ClaimTrack. If the account number is incomplete, open CardView and locate the matching account using the last four digits and customer name.

  4. Review the claim narrative.
    Read the customer’s statement in full. Confirm whether the issue is described as:

  5. debit card point-of-sale
  6. ATM
  7. ACH debit
  8. credit card billing error
  9. prepaid card transaction

  10. other disputed transaction

  11. Check transaction detail in CardView.
    In CardView, open the account history and locate the transaction date, amount, merchant name, and authorization information. Compare these details to the online form. If the customer lists a transaction that is not present in CardView, verify whether the item is pending, posted, or already reversed.

  12. Validate card processor information in Corvane.
    For card disputes, open the Corvane Card Services portal and search the transaction using the card account reference or transaction identifier. Confirm the network status, merchant descriptor, and any processor notes that affect dispute classification.

  13. Confirm the claim can be routed.
    Determine whether the claim is complete enough to proceed to intake classification. If required information is missing, update the ClaimTrack notes and route the item for follow-up per intake workflow.

  14. Enter review notes in ClaimTrack.
    Add a concise note stating:

  15. the form was reviewed
  16. the transaction details were verified in CardView and, if applicable, Corvane
  17. any discrepancies found

  18. whether the claim is ready for classification or needs correction

  19. Set the case disposition.
    Mark the claim as Ready for Classification, Needs Customer Follow-Up, or Return for Data Correction based on the review result. Save the case and confirm the record moves to the next queue.

  20. Close the review task.
    Ensure all screens are closed, no unsaved comments remain, and the claim appears in the correct ClaimTrack queue.

Tip

If the online form includes multiple transactions, review each transaction separately in CardView before selecting the disposition.

Warning

Do not file a dispute based solely on the online form text when the account history or Corvane record does not support the transaction details. Escalate the discrepancy through the normal intake follow-up process.

Tip

Use a short, factual note style. Avoid interpretations, promises to the customer, or references to outcome before the claim is classified.
