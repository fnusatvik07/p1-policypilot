Closing a claim: error found

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2017-04-01 · Version 1

WI-019 — Closing a Claim: Error Found

Type: Work Instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Effective/Issued Date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active
Version: 1

Purpose

Use this procedure when you are working a dispute claim and identify an error that requires the claim to be closed in ClaimTrack. This includes cases where the issue was entered under the wrong claim type, tied to the wrong account, or otherwise cannot proceed in the current workstream. Follow this instruction before finalizing the closure and routing any follow-up action to the appropriate queue.

Before you start

  • Confirm you are on the correct customer record and claim number.
  • Review the claim summary, coverage code, and transaction details in ClaimTrack.
  • Have CardView open for the account status check.
  • If the transaction is a processor item, open Corvane Card Services portal before closing the claim.
  • Do not close a claim until you have confirmed the error and documented the reason in the case notes.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack.
    Search by claim number, card number, or customer identifier. Open the claim from the active queue.

  2. Review the claim status and work history.
    Check the claim timeline, previous notes, and any assigned analyst activity. Confirm that no final determination has already been issued.

  3. Identify the error that prevents continuation.
    Common examples include:

  4. wrong account selected
  5. duplicate claim already in process
  6. incorrect transaction date or amount
  7. claim was opened under the wrong channel or claim type

  8. processor-side item that requires Corvane review under a different workflow

  9. Verify account and transaction details in CardView.
    Open CardView and confirm the account number, card status, and transaction posting details. If the account is closed, restricted, or moved, record that fact in the case notes.

  10. Check Corvane Card Services if the item is processor-managed.
    For debit, credit, or prepaid card transactions handled through Corvane, open the corresponding transaction inquiry in the Corvane portal. Confirm whether the item is already matched, reversed, charged back, or pending processor action.

  11. Enter a clear closure note in ClaimTrack.
    Use a concise statement that explains the error found and why the claim cannot proceed. Include the source of confirmation, such as CardView or Corvane portal review. Keep the note factual and specific.

  12. Select the correct closure reason.
    In ClaimTrack, choose the closure reason that matches the error type. If more than one reason appears applicable, use the one that best reflects the root cause and the next required action.

  13. Remove the claim from active work, if permitted by the queue rules.
    Update the case status to closed. If the claim needs reassignment instead of closure, route it to the correct queue and leave the claim open only when required by process.

  14. Save the record and confirm the closed status.
    Refresh the claim to verify the status change, closure reason, and notes were saved successfully.

  15. File any supporting evidence.
    Attach screenshots, transaction details, or Corvane responses if required by the claim record. Refer to the retention requirements in DOP-229 — Claim Records and Evidence Retention.

Tips

Tip: If the error is caused by a duplicate claim, check DOP-223 — Duplicate and Repeat Claims before closing the record. Duplicate claims often require a specific closure reason and note format.

Tip: If the customer should be notified of the closure reason, confirm the notice requirement in DOP-212 — Final Determination and Customer Notices and DOP-230 — Customer Letters and Notices before sending any communication.

Warnings

Warning: Do not close a claim based on assumption. The closure reason must match what you verified in ClaimTrack, CardView, or Corvane.

Warning: If the claim appears to involve fraud, account compromise, or unauthorized activity, stop and route to the appropriate process in DOP-218 — Fraud Referral and Account Compromise.
