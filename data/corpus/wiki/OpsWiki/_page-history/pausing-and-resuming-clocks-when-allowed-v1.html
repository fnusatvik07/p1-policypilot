WI-013 — Pausing and Resuming Clocks (When Allowed)

Type: Work instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 1
Effective / issued date: 2017-04-01
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

This page explains how Claims Intake and Investigations associates pause and resume a claim clock in ClaimTrack, CardView, or Corvane Card Services when the pause is permitted under the applicable procedure. Use this page with the claim-type procedure and DOP-210 — Investigation Timelines and Extensions. Do not pause a clock unless the file meets an approved hold reason and the supporting note is entered in the case record.

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim is open and assigned to your queue.
  • Confirm the pause reason is allowed by the applicable procedure.
  • Have the customer contact note, merchant documentation, or internal request available before you change the clock status.
  • Use the correct case system for the product:
  • ClaimTrack for case management and clock status
  • CardView for card account review
  • Corvane Card Services for processor-level chargeback activity
  • If the case is already closed, do not reopen it solely to change a clock status. Route to your manager or the assigned analyst.

Pausing a clock in ClaimTrack

  1. Open ClaimTrack and sign in with your standard network credentials.
  2. Search the case by claim number, card number, or customer name.
  3. Open the case and select the Timeline tab.
  4. Review the existing clock status and confirm that the file supports a pause.
  5. Select Actions > Pause Clock.
  6. In the Pause Clock dialog, choose the applicable reason from the drop-down list.
  7. Enter a concise note describing why the pause is allowed and what document or event supports it.
  8. Attach the supporting file if the document is available in electronic form.
  9. Select Submit.
  10. Verify that the case banner updates to Clock Paused and that the pause appears in the activity history.

Tip: If the reason is not available in the drop-down list, stop and review the claim-type procedure. Do not select the nearest match.

Pausing a clock in CardView

  1. Open CardView and locate the customer account.
  2. Open the disputed transaction or card dispute summary.
  3. Select the Dispute Management or Case Notes section, depending on your screen layout.
  4. Check whether ClaimTrack already shows a paused clock.
  5. If the clock has not been paused, select Update Status > Pause Clock.
  6. Enter the pause reason and the supporting note.
  7. Save the record.
  8. Return to ClaimTrack and confirm the status synchronized correctly.

Warning: CardView is a servicing view. Always complete the official status change in ClaimTrack unless the procedure specifically instructs you otherwise.

Pausing a chargeback clock in Corvane

  1. Open the Corvane Card Services portal.
  2. Search for the dispute by ARN, card number, or case reference.
  3. Open the chargeback record.
  4. Select Case Actions > Hold / Pause.
  5. Choose the approved hold code.
  6. Add a brief internal note that matches the ClaimTrack narrative.
  7. Save the change.
  8. Return to ClaimTrack and update the case activity to match the Corvane record.

Resuming a clock

  1. Open the case in the system where the pause was entered.
  2. Review the reason for the hold and confirm the event that ends the pause has occurred.
  3. Select Actions > Resume Clock.
  4. Enter the date the pause ended and a brief note describing the trigger for resumption.
  5. Save the record.
  6. Check the activity history to confirm the clock resumed and the elapsed time calculation updated.

Tip: Resume the clock on the same business day the hold ends whenever the case is in active handling, so the file history stays aligned across systems.

If the clock will not resume

If the system rejects the update, verify the case is not locked for QA review, chargeback filing, or a duplicate-claim hold. If the issue remains, notify your lead and document the error code in the case notes. Do not create a second case to work around the lock.
