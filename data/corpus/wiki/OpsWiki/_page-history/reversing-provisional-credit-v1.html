Reversing provisional credit

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2017-04-01 · Version 1

WI-021 — Reversing provisional credit

Type: Work instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective/Issued: 2017-04-01
Status: Active

Purpose

Use this procedure when a dispute case has an outstanding provisional credit and the investigator or supervisor has approved reversal. This page covers the operational steps to document the reversal, update the card account record, and complete the Corvane return action where applicable. For decisioning and customer notice requirements, see DOP-213 — Reversal of Provisional Credit and DOP-212 — Final Determination and Customer Notices.

Before you start

  • Confirm the case is in a status that allows reversal under DOP-213.
  • Verify the approved reversal amount in the case notes and supporting documentation.
  • Confirm whether the provisional credit was posted to a debit, credit, or prepaid card account.
  • If the case involves a network chargeback or processor action, have the Corvane case reference available.
  • Do not reverse partial amounts unless the approval specifically authorizes a partial reversal.

Procedure

  1. Open the case in ClaimTrack.
    Search by case number, customer name, or card number. Open the case and review the Investigation Summary, Timeline, and Financials tabs before making any changes.

  2. Confirm the reversal basis.
    Check the case disposition and notes to verify that the provisional credit must be reversed. If the disposition is unclear, stop and route the case to the assigned analyst or supervisor.

  3. Select the provisional credit transaction.
    In the Financials tab, locate the provisional credit entry. Confirm the posting date, amount, and linked account. Record these details in the case notes.

  4. Initiate the reversal in ClaimTrack.
    Choose Actions > Reverse Provisional Credit. Enter the approved reversal amount exactly as shown in the approval record. Add a brief reason code and any required comment text.

  5. Submit the accounting update.
    Save the transaction and verify that ClaimTrack shows the reversal as pending completion or posted, depending on the case configuration.

  6. Update CardView.
    Open CardView for the affected cardholder account. Review the ledger to confirm the provisional credit appears on the account history. If the reversal has not yet reflected, use the account notes to document that the reversal was initiated in ClaimTrack.

  7. Post the offset or debit adjustment.
    In CardView, apply the reversal adjustment using the appropriate account maintenance function for the card type. Confirm the amount, effective date, and transaction description before submitting.

  8. Check for balance changes.
    Review the available balance, current balance, and any dispute-related holds. If the reversal creates a negative balance or overdraft condition, note it in the case and follow the related account servicing process.

  9. Complete Corvane action, if applicable.
    If the original provisional credit was tied to a Corvane-managed dispute or chargeback workflow, sign in to the Corvane Card Services portal. Locate the case using the Corvane reference number and post the reversal status update. Save the audit trail reference in ClaimTrack.

  10. Document the final outcome.
    Return to ClaimTrack and enter the final reversal note, including the CardView confirmation and any Corvane reference numbers. Attach supporting screenshots or system confirmations only if required by the case file standard.

  11. Close the work item.
    Mark the operational step complete and route the case to the next queue or closure step per DOP-212 and DOP-229 — Claim Records and Evidence Retention.

Tip

Keep the reversal note concise and factual. The record should show what was reversed, when it was reversed, and which system completed the final posting.

Warning

A reversal entered in ClaimTrack without the matching CardView adjustment can leave the account out of balance. Always verify both systems before closing the work item.

Tip

If Corvane shows a different amount than ClaimTrack, pause and reconcile the case before posting. Do not “correct” the amount in more than one system without an approved adjustment record.
