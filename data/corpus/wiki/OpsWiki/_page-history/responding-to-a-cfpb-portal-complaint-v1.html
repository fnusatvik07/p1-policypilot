WI-046 — Responding to a CFPB portal complaint

Type: Work Instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 1
Effective / issued date: 2017-04-01
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Overview

Use this work instruction when a complaint is received through the CFPB portal and the matter relates to a consumer dispute, card dispute, or dispute servicing activity handled by Disputes Operations. This page covers the operational workflow for opening the complaint, reviewing the underlying dispute record, coordinating the response, and closing the matter in the applicable systems.

This instruction supports the complaint-handling process in DOP-235 — Responding to CFPB Portal Complaints about Disputes and should be used with the dispute case file in ClaimTrack, account-level details in CardView, and merchant/network information in Corvane Card Services.

Before you start

  • Confirm the complaint is assigned to Disputes Operations and not another business line.
  • Gather the CFPB complaint number, customer name, account number, and dispute case ID.
  • Open ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane portal before beginning your review.
  • Do not edit the case narrative until you have reviewed the existing dispute notes and attachments.
  • If the complaint alleges employee conduct, suspected fraud, or broad servicing failure, stop and route per the applicable escalation path in DOP-228 and DOP-218.

Procedure

  1. Open the complaint record in ClaimTrack.
    Search by CFPB complaint number or customer name. Confirm the case is linked to the correct dispute file and product type. If the complaint is not already linked, create the link using the dispute case ID and save the record.

  2. Review the complaint summary and issue statement.
    Read the customer’s allegation carefully and compare it to the dispute type, claim date, and prior correspondence. Identify whether the complaint concerns transaction validity, provisional credit, merchant handling, or case timing.

  3. Pull the account view in CardView.
    Verify the card status, account ownership, and any recent changes to the account. Confirm whether a debit, credit, or prepaid product is involved. If the complaint references posted transactions or returned items, review the transaction history before proceeding.

  4. Check the network file in Corvane Card Services.
    Open the merchant or chargeback record tied to the disputed transaction. Confirm the chargeback stage, representment status, and any recovery activity. Use this information to validate the facts in ClaimTrack and to identify any missing support.

  5. Compare the complaint against the investigation record.
    Review notes, letters, evidence uploads, and prior contact attempts in ClaimTrack. Determine whether the original disposition, timeline, or customer notice is consistent with the complaint content. If a system entry is missing or unclear, add a concise internal note with the date, source, and reason for the update.

  6. Draft the complaint response.
    Prepare the operational response using the approved complaint workflow in ClaimTrack. Address the specific issue raised by the customer, cite the dispute outcome already recorded, and avoid introducing new facts that are not supported by the file. If the matter requires coordination with another unit, note the handoff and assigned owner.

  7. Route the draft for review if required.
    Send the response for supervisory review when the case involves complex evidence, a high-value dispute, a requested correction, or a potential policy exception. Use the standard internal queue for complaint review and retain the draft in the case file.

  8. Finalize and close the complaint record.
    After the response is approved and sent, update ClaimTrack with the final disposition, response date, and closure reason. Confirm that any related customer notice, evidence packet, or correspondence is attached. Then mark the complaint complete in the portal workflow.

Tip: Keep ClaimTrack notes factual and chronological. Use the account and case identifiers consistently so QA review can trace the complaint from intake to closure.

Warning: Do not close the complaint until you have confirmed that the dispute case, account file, and Corvane record all reflect the same final outcome.
