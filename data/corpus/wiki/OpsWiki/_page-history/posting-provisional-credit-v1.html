Posting provisional credit

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2017-04-01 · Version 1

WI-014 — Posting provisional credit

Type: Work instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective/issued date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this page to post provisional credit after a dispute claim is approved for temporary credit under the applicable investigation workflow. This instruction covers the operational steps in ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane portal, along with the validation checks required before the credit is released to the customer’s deposit or card account.

Before you start

Before you start
Confirm that the claim is eligible for provisional credit, the approval is recorded in ClaimTrack, and the account is open and not restricted from posting. If the customer holds multiple accounts, verify that the credit is being applied to the same account referenced in the claim record. Do not post provisional credit until the investigation owner has completed the approval step or a documented escalation authorizes release.

Prerequisites

  • Claim is in approved status in ClaimTrack
  • Account information is verified in CardView or HLX Core, as applicable
  • Corvane access is available for debit, credit, or prepaid card posting activity
  • You have the claim number, masked account number, and credit amount
  • If the account is joint, trust, deceased, or POA-related, confirm the case has the required handling notes before posting

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack.
    Search by claim number or customer identifier, then open the case from the active queue. Review the case summary, claim type, and approval notes. Confirm that the case status shows approved for provisional credit.

  2. Review the claim details.
    Check the dispute amount, product type, and posting instructions. Confirm whether the credit applies to a deposit account or a card account. If the claim includes multiple transactions, verify the exact amount approved for posting.

  3. Validate the target account in CardView.
    Open CardView and search using the masked card number or customer profile. Confirm the account status, product type, and available servicing profile. For deposit-linked debit disputes, confirm the checking account on record matches the claim.

  4. Open Corvane portal for the posting action.
    Launch the Corvane Card Services portal and navigate to the account servicing screen. Use the search function to locate the card or prepaid profile tied to the claim.

  5. Select the provisional credit function.
    From the account actions menu, choose Credit Adjustment or the equivalent provisional credit posting option. Enter the approved credit amount exactly as recorded in ClaimTrack. Do not round or alter the amount unless the claim note explicitly instructs otherwise.

  6. Enter transaction details.
    Populate the posting reason, claim number, and reference text. Use the standard description required by Disputes Operations. Include the approval date and your initials or operator ID in the comment field if the screen allows narrative notes.

  7. Confirm the funding account.
    If the credit posts to a deposit account, verify the destination account in HLX Core before final submission. If the credit posts to a credit or prepaid card ledger, confirm the correct Corvane profile is selected.

  8. Submit the posting.
    Review all fields on the confirmation screen, then submit the transaction. Wait for the system acknowledgment and save the confirmation number or posting ID.

  9. Update ClaimTrack.
    Return to ClaimTrack and record the posting confirmation number, amount, and posting date in the case notes. Change the claim task to reflect that provisional credit has been issued.

  10. Complete the customer record.
    If the workflow requires it, generate or queue the customer notice using the applicable notice process. Ensure the case file contains the posting confirmation and any exception notes.

Tips and warnings

Tip
Always compare the claim amount in ClaimTrack with the amount entered in Corvane before submitting. Most posting errors occur when analysts rely on memory instead of the approved case amount.

Warning
Do not post provisional credit to a closed, charged-off, or blocked account without documented approval from the investigation manager. If the system rejects the transaction, stop and escalate rather than retrying multiple times.

Tip
If the customer has received a merchant credit during the investigation, check the claim notes before posting to avoid duplicate crediting.
