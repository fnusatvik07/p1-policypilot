Purpose

This work instruction explains how to route a high-value claim for approval in ClaimTrack and, where applicable, record supporting account context in CardView and Corvane Card Services. Use this page after you have completed intake, classification, and initial coverage review under DOP-201, DOP-203, DOP-204, DOP-205, DOP-206, or DOP-209, and the claim meets the approval threshold or other escalation criteria described in DOP-219.

Before you start

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim is already opened in ClaimTrack and assigned to the correct queue.
  • Have the customer profile open in CardView if the claim involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card.
  • Use Corvane Card Services only for claims that require processor-side review or network chargeback coordination.
  • Confirm your access includes approval routing and note updates in ClaimTrack.
  • Do not route a claim until the claim type, product, and dispute reason are confirmed.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack.
    Search by claim number, customer name, or account number. Open the claim summary and review the Status, Claim Type, Product, and Assigned Queue fields.

  2. Verify the approval trigger.
    In the case header, review the dollar amount, duplicate claim flag, prior provisional credit activity, and any manual review notes. If the case is already designated for high-value routing, the Approval Routing banner displays at the top of the claim.

  3. Select the routing action.
    In the left navigation, click Actions > Route for Approval.
    In the routing window, select the appropriate approval path:

  4. Claims Intake Supervisor
  5. Investigations Manager
  6. Chargeback Recovery Manager

  7. QA Review Hold

  8. Enter the routing reason.
    In Reason Code, choose the code that matches the case condition. In Comments, enter a concise summary of the routing basis, including the claim type, amount, and any exception factors. Keep comments factual and limited to case handling.

  9. Attach supporting notes or documents.
    If the claim file includes a customer affidavit, merchant correspondence, or supporting screenshots, click Attachments and confirm the items are present. If a document is missing, note the gap in the comments field rather than uploading an incomplete file set.

  10. Update CardView, if needed.
    Open CardView in a new tab. Review the account status, card product, and recent posting history. If there is a temporary service issue, card reissue indicator, or cardholder restriction that affects routing, record the note in ClaimTrack before returning to the routing screen.

  11. Check Corvane Card Services, if applicable.
    For claims involving network chargeback activity, open the corresponding Corvane case. Confirm merchant response dates, provisional debit activity, and processor notes. If Corvane shows a pending action, enter a reference note in ClaimTrack and continue routing.

  12. Submit the approval route.
    Return to ClaimTrack, confirm the selected path, and click Submit. Verify the case status changes to Pending Approval or the equivalent approval-hold status shown by the queue configuration.

  13. Notify the next queue.
    Use the internal task note to indicate the routing destination and any time-sensitive handling point. If the case has a same-day customer contact requirement, include that in the queue note.

  14. Close the routing task.
    Confirm the activity log shows the route action, timestamp, and user ID. If the case remains on hold, leave the case open and stop processing until approval is completed.

Tip

If the claim is borderline and you are unsure whether it requires approval routing, pause and escalate through the queue lead before submitting. Incorrect routing can delay customer communication and downstream case handling.

Warning

Do not edit the claim amount, dispute reason, or product type to force a different approval path. Route the case based on the verified record only.
