WI-018 — Handling chargeback representments

Overview

This work instruction explains how Disputes Operations associates process chargeback representments after a merchant re-submits documentation through Corvane Card Services. The procedure covers review of the supporting evidence, case updates in ClaimTrack, account verification in CardView, and filing the representment response in the Corvane portal.

This page is used by Chargeback Recovery and by analysts supporting debit and credit card disputes when a representment is received for an existing chargeback case. Follow the steps in the order shown to ensure the case record, customer impact, and filing status remain aligned.

[!note] Before you start - Confirm the case is already opened in ClaimTrack and is assigned to a chargeback recovery queue. - Have the ClaimTrack case number, cardholder name, last four digits of the card, and merchant name available. - Review the chargeback reason code and the original dispute summary before opening the merchant packet. - Use CardView to confirm the current card status and account ownership before taking any action on the case. - If the merchant packet is incomplete, route the case to the queue lead for review rather than filing a partial response.

Procedure

  1. Open the case in ClaimTrack
  2. Sign in to ClaimTrack.
  3. In the search field, enter the ClaimTrack case number.
  4. Open the case from the results list.

  5. Confirm the case status is Chargeback Pending Representment or the equivalent recovery status used on the queue.

  6. Review the case record

  7. Select the Dispute Details tab.
  8. Verify the merchant name, transaction date, amount, and reason code.
  9. Review the notes section for prior customer contact, provisional credit activity, and any prior representment actions.

  10. Open the document list and confirm whether the merchant packet has already been attached.

  11. Check the cardholder profile in CardView

  12. Launch CardView from the case workspace.
  13. Search by card number or customer identifier.
  14. Confirm the card is linked to the correct account.
  15. Verify the account status, card status, and mailing address on file.
  16. If the account is closed or the card has been reissued, document the status in ClaimTrack before proceeding.

[!warning] Do not update the case outcome until you have reviewed the cardholder profile and confirmed that the representment packet matches the transaction on file. Filing against the wrong transaction can create an inaccurate recovery record.

  1. Open the representment packet in Corvane
  2. Select Open in Corvane from the ClaimTrack case toolbar.
  3. Sign in to the Corvane Card Services portal if prompted.
  4. Locate the merchant submission associated with the case number.
  5. Open the attached images, receipts, logs, or other evidence supplied by the merchant.

  6. Compare the submission date and transaction details against the ClaimTrack record.

  7. Assess the merchant evidence

  8. Determine whether the merchant documentation supports the original chargeback reason.
  9. Confirm that all pages are legible and that signatures, authorization data, or delivery evidence are present when required.
  10. If the packet is incomplete or unrelated to the disputed transaction, mark the submission as insufficient and note the deficiency.
  11. If the packet supports the merchant, prepare the case for representment acceptance.

[!tip] Use the case notes template in ClaimTrack for concise documentation: - source of the merchant packet - evidence reviewed - result of the review - next action taken in Corvane Consistent notes reduce rework during QA review.

  1. Update the case in ClaimTrack
  2. Return to ClaimTrack and open the Case Actions menu.
  3. Select the appropriate outcome for the review.
  4. Enter the review summary in the notes field.
  5. Attach any internal reference documents used in the determination.

  6. Save the case record and verify the status change appears on the main case screen.

  7. Submit the representment action in Corvane

  8. Return to the Corvane portal.
  9. Select the filing or response action required by the case.
  10. Upload the case reference number and any required internal response fields.
  11. Confirm the submission details before finalizing.

  12. Record the Corvane confirmation number in ClaimTrack notes.

  13. Close out the work item

  14. Reopen the ClaimTrack case summary.
  15. Confirm the case reflects the submitted representment action.
  16. Check for any pending tasks, missing documents, or exceptions.
  17. If no additional action is required, release the case to the next queue or complete the work item per queue instructions.

[!important] If the merchant packet arrives after the case has already been advanced to the next stage, do not overwrite prior work without supervisor review. Preserve the original record and document the late arrival in the case notes.
