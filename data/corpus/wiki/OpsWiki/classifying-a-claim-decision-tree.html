WI-007 — Classifying a claim: decision tree

Type: Work Instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 2
Effective / issued date: 2023-06-05
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Purpose

Use this procedure to classify a consumer dispute claim into the correct claim type before investigation work is assigned. Accurate classification ensures the claim is routed to the appropriate work queue, coverage review is applied consistently, and downstream notices reflect the correct product and transaction type.

This page is used by Claims Intake associates in ClaimTrack 2.0, with reference to CardView and Corvane Card Services as needed.

Before you start

  • Confirm customer identity and claim intake details are already complete per CPS-SOP-201.
  • Open the claim in ClaimTrack 2.0 and verify the product, transaction date, and transaction channel.
  • If the claim involves a debit, credit, or prepaid card transaction, have CardView available.
  • If the transaction is chargeback-eligible or a network item, be prepared to open Corvane Card Services.
  • Do not classify a claim based only on the customer’s description. Use the account and transaction record first.
  • Use the automated deadline clocks in ClaimTrack 2.0 as a reference only; classification is based on the record, not the clock status.

Procedure

  1. In ClaimTrack 2.0, open the customer’s claim from the Open Claims queue.
  2. Review the Claim Summary panel and confirm:
  3. account number,
  4. disputed amount,
  5. transaction date,
  6. product type,
  7. channel of receipt.
  8. Select Transactions to display the disputed item list.
  9. Compare the disputed item against the account record in CardView:
  10. Click Open CardView from the claim record.
  11. Search by card number or masked account number.
  12. Open the most recent card account page.
  13. Verify whether the transaction is debit, credit, or prepaid.
  14. Determine whether the dispute is:
  15. a card purchase,
  16. an ATM cash withdrawal,
  17. an ACH debit,
  18. a billing error,
  19. a network chargeback item,
  20. or a duplicate / repeat claim.
  21. In ClaimTrack 2.0, open Classification and select the first-level claim type:
  22. Debit Card POS
  23. ATM
  24. ACH Debit
  25. Credit Card Billing Error
  26. Chargeback / Merchant Dispute
  27. Other / Refer
  28. If the claim is a card dispute processed through Corvane:
  29. Click Launch Corvane Portal from the claim record.
  30. Locate the transaction using the ARN, trace number, or transaction date.
  31. Confirm whether the item is still within network processing scope.
  32. Record the Corvane status in the claim notes.
  33. If the claim involves multiple transactions, classify each transaction line separately in ClaimTrack.
  34. If the claim appears to involve fraud, a compromised account, or an unauthorized transfer, mark Potential Fraud and route according to CPS-SOP-218 and CPS-SOP-203.
  35. If the claim cannot be matched to a supported claim type, select Refer for Review and add a concise note stating the reason.
  36. Save the classification.
  37. Reopen the claim summary and confirm the work queue has updated correctly.
  38. Add a case note that includes:
  39. selected claim type,
  40. source system reviewed,
  41. any discrepancy between customer description and transaction record,
  42. any referral or exception.

Tip

Use the transaction record first, then the customer narrative. Inconsistent descriptions are common during intake and do not by themselves determine the claim type.

Warning

Do not assign a chargeback classification unless the transaction is actually card-network eligible. If uncertain, refer the claim instead of forcing a category.

Classification cues

If the transaction is… Start with… Supporting page
Card purchase disputed as unauthorized or incorrect Credit or debit card classification CPS-SOP-204 / CPS-SOP-209
Cash withdrawal at an ATM ATM classification CPS-SOP-205
Electronic debit from deposit account ACH debit classification CPS-SOP-206
Customer says a card charge is incorrect but not unauthorized Billing error classification CPS-SOP-209
Network dispute on debit, credit, or prepaid card Corvane review CPS-SOP-217 / CPS-SOP-234

Tip

If a claim spans more than one product, classify the primary transaction first and keep the secondary transaction in the notes for downstream review.
