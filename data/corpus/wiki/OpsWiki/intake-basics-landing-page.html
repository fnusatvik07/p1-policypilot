Purpose

Use this page as the entry point for dispute and claims intake work. It is designed for quick navigation, not procedure detail. For step-by-step instructions, use the linked DOP procedures and the deadline reference currently in circulation.

Where to start

  1. Identify the intake source. Determine whether the claim came from the contact center, branch, online/mobile channel, or a back-office referral.
  2. Confirm the product and transaction type. Use the relevant product-specific procedure before taking any customer commitment.
  3. Open or update the case. ClaimTrack is the system of record for dispute intake and case tracking.
  4. Check whether the matter is time-sensitive. Use the deadline reference and the applicable procedure set before promising timing to the customer.
  5. Route exceptions promptly. High-value, fraud-related, elder exploitation, and complaint-linked claims require immediate attention under the applicable DOP process.
