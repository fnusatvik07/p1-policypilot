Closing a claim: no error found

Created by Darnell Price · Last updated 2017-04-01 · Version 1

WI-020 — Closing a claim: no error found

Type: Work Instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 1
Effective/issued date: 2017-04-01
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this procedure when the investigation is complete and the analyst has determined that no error occurred on the customer’s account. This work instruction covers the final system updates, customer disposition coding, and claim closure steps in ClaimTrack, with supporting review in CardView and, where applicable, Corvane Card Services.

Before you start

Before you start

  • Confirm the claim is fully investigated and the evidence supports a no error found decision.
  • Confirm any related card-account activity has been reviewed in CardView and any processor records, if applicable, in Corvane Card Services.
  • Confirm the claim is not waiting on merchant documentation, customer documents, or a pending fraud referral.
  • If the case includes a card network chargeback, follow the applicable chargeback workflow before closing the claim.
  • Use the correct claim type and product code before finalizing the disposition.

Procedure

  1. Open the claim in ClaimTrack.
    Search for the claim number from the Claims Work Queue and open the claim record. Review the case summary, channel, product type, and current status before making any changes.

  2. Review the investigation notes.
    In the Notes or Activity tab, confirm the investigation summary states that no billing error, unauthorized transfer, or posting error was identified. Verify that the final disposition is consistent with the evidence already saved to the file.

  3. Check the account details in CardView.
    Open CardView and review the relevant card account activity, transaction posting history, and available account comments. Confirm the transaction date, amount, merchant or network information, and any prior adjustment activity.

  4. Review Corvane records, if the claim was card-related.
    Open the applicable case or transaction record in the Corvane Card Services portal. Confirm the processor record aligns with the ClaimTrack investigation results, including any network response, reversals, or prior chargeback action.

  5. Return to ClaimTrack and update the disposition.
    In the claim decision section, select the final result indicating no error found. Enter the decision note in clear operational language, including the reason the claim is being closed. Do not use shorthand that is not supported by the case record.

  6. Confirm provisional credit status.
    If provisional credit was issued earlier in the investigation, verify whether a reversal is required before closure. If a reversal applies, complete that action in the applicable workflow before finalizing the claim.

  7. Add the closure note.
    In the final notes field, summarize the evidence reviewed, the system checks completed, and the reason for closure. Keep the entry factual and specific. Example structure:

  8. account reviewed in CardView
  9. processor data reviewed in Corvane, if applicable
  10. no error identified

  11. claim closed as no error found

  12. Update the claim status to closed.
    Change the status from open or pending to closed. Save the record and confirm ClaimTrack shows the final disposition on the claim header.

  13. Verify the customer notice queue.
    Confirm the claim is routed to the correct notice or correspondence queue for the final customer communication. If the case requires a notice, ensure the notice type matches the claim disposition before releasing the file.

  14. File supporting documentation.
    Attach or confirm all supporting evidence is saved in the claim record. If additional documents were used during the review, make sure they are linked to the closed case record before exiting.

Tips

Tip

Use the claim notes to show the decision path, not just the result. A complete closure note helps Quality Assurance and reduces rework during post-review.

Tip

When Corvane records and CardView activity do not match, pause closure and return the case to the appropriate investigation queue.

Warnings

Warning

Do not close the claim if a fraud referral, merchant credit, or duplicate claim review is still pending.

Warning

Do not change the disposition after closure unless you are instructed to reopen the claim under an approved correction workflow.
