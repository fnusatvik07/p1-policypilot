WI-046 — Responding to a CFPB portal complaint

Type: Work Instruction
Status at issue: Active
Version: 2
Effective / issued date: 2023-06-05
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training

Overview

Use this work instruction when a complaint is received through the CFPB portal and the matter relates to a consumer dispute, card dispute, or dispute servicing activity handled by Disputes Operations. This page covers the operational workflow for opening the complaint, reviewing the underlying dispute record, coordinating the response, and closing the matter in the applicable systems.

This version is updated for ClaimTrack 2.0, including the automated deadline clocks used in complaint and dispute servicing workflows. Use this page with CPS-SOP-235 — Responding to CFPB Portal Complaints about Disputes, the dispute case file in ClaimTrack, account-level details in CardView, and merchant/network information in Corvane Card Services.

Before you start

  • Confirm the complaint is assigned to Disputes Operations and not another business line.
  • Gather the CFPB complaint number, customer name, account number, and dispute case ID.
  • Open ClaimTrack, CardView, and the Corvane portal before beginning your review.
  • Review the ClaimTrack 2.0 deadline clock before making any case edits.
  • Do not edit the case narrative until you have reviewed the existing dispute notes and attachments.
  • If the complaint alleges employee conduct, suspected fraud, or broad servicing failure, stop and route per the applicable escalation path in CPS-SOP-228 and CPS-SOP-218.

Procedure

  1. Open the complaint record in ClaimTrack.
    From the ClaimTrack home page, select Complaints from the left navigation pane. Search by CFPB complaint number or customer name. Open the complaint record and confirm the case is linked to the correct dispute file and product type. If the complaint is not already linked, select Related Cases, then Link Case, enter the dispute case ID, and save the record.

  2. Review the complaint summary and issue statement.
    Select the Summary tab and read the customer’s allegation carefully. Compare it to the dispute type, claim date, and prior correspondence. In ClaimTrack 2.0, verify the deadline clock at the top of the case screen before proceeding. Identify whether the complaint concerns transaction validity, provisional credit, merchant handling, or case timing.

  3. Pull the account view in CardView.
    Open CardView in a separate browser tab. Search by account number and select the correct household or cardholder profile. Verify the card status, account ownership, and any recent changes to the account. Confirm whether a debit, credit, or prepaid product is involved. If the complaint references posted transactions or returned items, review the transaction history before proceeding.

  4. Check the network file in Corvane Card Services.
    Launch the Corvane portal and open the merchant or chargeback record tied to the disputed transaction. Confirm the chargeback stage, representment status, and any recovery activity. Use the Case History and Documents views to validate the facts in ClaimTrack and to identify any missing support. Record only objective findings in the case notes.

  5. Compare the complaint against the investigation record.
    Return to ClaimTrack and review notes, letters, evidence uploads, and prior contact attempts. Determine whether the original disposition, timeline, or customer notice is consistent with the complaint content. If a system entry is missing or unclear, add a concise internal note with the date, source, and reason for the update. Use the Add Note function rather than overwriting prior entries.

  6. Draft the complaint response.
    Select Prepare Response in ClaimTrack and use the approved complaint workflow. Address the specific issue raised by the customer, cite the dispute outcome already recorded, and avoid introducing new facts that are not supported by the file. If the matter requires coordination with another unit, note the handoff and assigned owner. Save the draft response before routing.

  7. Route the draft for review if required.
    If supervisory review is needed, select Send for Review and route the case to the designated queue. Use the standard internal complaint review queue and retain the draft in the case file. Cases involving complex evidence, a high-value dispute, or a requested correction should remain in review until the reviewer marks the case Approved.

  8. Finalize and close the complaint record.
    After the response is approved and sent, update ClaimTrack with the final disposition, response date, and closure reason. Confirm that any related customer notice, evidence packet, or correspondence is attached in the Documents tab. Then select Close Complaint in the portal workflow. Verify that the case status changes to Closed and that the deadline clock is no longer active.

Tip: Keep ClaimTrack notes factual and chronological. Use the account and case identifiers consistently so QA review can trace the complaint from intake to closure.

Warning: Do not close the complaint until you have confirmed that the dispute case, account file, and Corvane record all reflect the same final outcome.
