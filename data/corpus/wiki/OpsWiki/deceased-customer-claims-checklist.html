WI-033 — Deceased customer claims checklist

Type: Work instruction
System(s): ClaimTrack 2.0, CardView, Corvane Card Services portal
Owner: Darnell Price, Manager, Operations Training
Version: 2
Effective/Issued: 2023-06-05
Status at issue: Active

Purpose

Use this checklist when a claim is received for a deceased customer and must be handled under CPS-SOP-220 — Claims on Behalf of Deceased Customers and Agents (POA). This page sets out the standard sequence for locating the account, opening the claim in ClaimTrack 2.0, and documenting deceased-customer indicators in the servicing systems.

Before you start

  • Confirm the caller, branch associate, or back-office submitter is requesting action on behalf of a deceased customer.
  • Confirm whether the claim relates to a debit, credit, or prepaid card account before you open the case.
  • Gather the customer name, last four digits of the card or account, date of death if provided, and any death documentation already received.
  • Do not release account information until you have completed identity and authority checks per CPS-SOP-220 and the applicable product terms.

Checklist

  1. Search for the customer in ClaimTrack 2.0
  2. Open ClaimTrack 2.0.
  3. Select Search from the top navigation bar.
  4. Enter the customer name and, if available, the last four digits of the account or card number.
  5. Review the results list for an existing claim, an open deceased-customer flag, or a prior note.

  6. If an open claim already exists, open the case and continue with step 4. If no claim exists, select New Claim.

  7. Verify the account type in CardView

  8. From the ClaimTrack customer record, select Open CardView.
  9. Confirm whether the account is debit, credit, or prepaid.
  10. Review the account status, card status, and recent activity.
  11. Check for any deceased-customer indicator, stop card, or account restriction already placed.
  12. Return to ClaimTrack and add relevant status notes to the case narrative.

Tip

If the submitter provided only a card number, CardView usually returns the account faster than a general claim search. Use the card number first, then confirm the customer record in ClaimTrack.

  1. Confirm claim authority
  2. Review the submitted documents for a death certificate, executor papers, personal representative papers, or power of attorney records.
  3. Compare the documentation to the information in the case file.
  4. If authority is incomplete, select Follow-Up Required and document the missing item in ClaimTrack.

  5. If authority is confirmed, proceed to claim classification.

  6. Classify the claim in ClaimTrack

  7. Select Claim Type and choose the appropriate dispute category.
  8. Add the deceased-customer indicator in the case comments.
  9. Note whether the claim concerns a transaction dispute, billing error, or unauthorized transfer.
  10. If the account is a debit or prepaid card, continue to the Corvane review in step 5.

  11. If the account is a credit card, document the card product and skip to step 6.

  12. Review processor records in Corvane Card Services

  13. Open the Corvane Card Services portal.
  14. Search using the card number and transaction date.
  15. Review posted transactions, pending reversals, and network chargeback status.
  16. Capture any merchant response or chargeback reference number in ClaimTrack.
  17. If Corvane shows a pending item already in process, add a note and do not create a duplicate case.

Warning

Do not submit a second dispute for the same transaction unless the prior item has been formally closed and the case notes support a new review.

  1. Document the case and route
  2. Enter a clear summary in ClaimTrack, including the authority reviewed, account type, and any system notes from CardView or Corvane.
  3. Attach any death documentation received through approved intake channels.
  4. Route the claim to the appropriate investigations queue based on product type and claim classification.

  5. If the case involves a branch-submitted packet or contact center intake, ensure the original channel is referenced in the narrative.

  6. Close the intake checklist

  7. Confirm the case has a complete narrative and the deceased-customer indicator is present.
  8. Verify all system notes are saved.
  9. Return any incomplete authority items to the submitter using the standard follow-up path.
  10. Exit the case in ClaimTrack.
