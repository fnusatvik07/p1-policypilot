# Remittance transfers

Resources to help industry participants understand, implement, and comply with the remittance transfer rule (subpart B of Regulation E).

### Featured topic

On May 12, 2025, the CFPB withdrew several guidance documents, including “Statement regarding supervisory and enforcement practices in light of the COV1D-19 pandemic,” and “CFPB Bulletin 2012-08: Remittance transfer rule implementation.” You can read more about the withdrawal [here](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/12/2025-08286/interpretive-rules-policy-statements-and-advisory-opinions-withdrawal).

On September 20, 2024, [the Bureau proposed](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/rules-under-development/remittance-transfers-under-the-electronic-fund-transfer-act-regulation-e/) a narrowly tailored amendment to certain remittance transfer disclosure requirements and accompanying model forms under Regulation E.

## Regulations and official interpretations

**Browse the Remittance Rule (Subpart B of Regulation E, 12 CFR 1005) on:** [Interactive Bureau Regulations](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1005/) | [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/cgi-bin/text-idx?SID=d35eac868c3a288d6a85ca2bd0f33e40&mc=true&node=pt12.8.1005&rgn=div5#sp12.8.1005.b)

Main remittance transfer rule provisions and official interpretations can be found in:

- § 1005.30, Definitions
- § 1005.31, Disclosures
- § 1005.32, Estimates
- § 1005.33, Error resolution
- § 1005.34, Cancellation and refund of remittance transfers
- § 1005.35, Acts of agents
- § 1005.36, Transfers scheduled before date of transfer
- Appendix A, Model disclosure clauses and forms
