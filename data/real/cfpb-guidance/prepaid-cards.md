# Prepaid cards

Resources to help industry participants understand, implement, and comply with the Prepaid Rule.

### Featured topic

On April 29, 2022, the Bureau released [Spanish translations](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/consumer-cards-resources/prepaid-cards/prepaid-model-forms-samples/) of Prepaid Model Forms.

On February 15, 2022, the Bureau issued a [Compliance Bulletin](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_bulletin-2022-02_electronic-fund-transfer-act_2022-02.pdf) on the Electronic Fund Transfer Act’s (EFTA’s) compulsory use prohibition. Section 913 of EFTA provides, among other things, that no person may require a consumer to establish an account for receipt of electronic fund transfers with a particular financial institution as a condition of receipt of a government benefit. The Bureau issued the Compliance Bulletin to reiterate that this prohibition in EFTA applies to government benefit accounts.

On April 13, 2020, the Bureau issued an [interpretive rule](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_interpretive-rule_pandemic-relief-payments-reg-e.pdf) on pandemic relief payments.
